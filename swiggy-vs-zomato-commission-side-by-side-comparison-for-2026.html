Swiggy commission for cloud kitchens: what is different?

There is no separate published Swiggy commission rate for cloud kitchens. A delivery-only kitchen signs the same kind of agreement as any other restaurant. What changes is how hard the cost hits.

  • Every rupee of revenue carries commission. A dine-in restaurant can balance delivery costs against table sales. A cloud kitchen has no other channel to lean on.
  • New kitchens have the least leverage. With no order history, you usually sign the rate you are offered. Read the agreement before you go live, not after.
  • The onboarding fee comes first. Swiggy's annual report confirms a one-time, non-refundable onboarding fee. Ask for the amount in writing.
  • Radius matters on Zomato. Kitchens that serve a wide delivery radius get more far-off orders. Zomato's distance fees can lift cost on exactly those orders.
  • Discount funding is the swing factor. A kitchen that funds 100% of every offer pays far more per order than one that shares the cost.

Checklist: how to audit your Swiggy and Zomato commission

  1. Find your signed commission or service fee rate in your agreement or enrolment form. Write it down for each platform.
  2. Pull one recent payout statement from each platform and list every deduction line. Our payout statement guide explains each line.
  3. Confirm the collection fee on Swiggy and the payment mechanism fee on Zomato, and check GST is charged only once on each.
  4. Look for a Swiggy One fee line. Only about 1% of partners were reported to be covered, so check before you assume.
  5. Count Zomato orders with long-distance fees. If many come from beyond 4 km, review your delivery radius and menu for those areas.
  6. Match the monthly invoices against payouts. Zomato's terms say it issues a monthly invoice within seven days of month end.
  7. Check your TDS on the service fee. Zomato's terms ask restaurant partners to deposit it and give a refund route if the fee was already withheld.
  8. Get every discount funding split in writing, including Zomato Gold and Swiggy-shared offers.
  9. Work out effective cost per order, with ads and discounts included, for each platform.
  10. Read every fee-change notice in your partner app. On Zomato, changes can take effect seven days after notice.
