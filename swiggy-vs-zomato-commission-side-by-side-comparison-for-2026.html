Swiggy and Zomato charge similar base commission, typically 18–28% of order value in the contracts we audit, and press reports say about 16–30%. The add-ons differ: both charge a payment or collection fee of about 2%, Swiggy adds a Swiggy One fee for some partners, and Zomato adds a long-distance fee. Effective cost usually lands around 25–40%.

Once GST, payment fees, advertising and discounts are included, the gap between the two platforms is small. The real difference usually lies in:

  • Ad efficiency
  • Order volume
  • Conversion rate
  • Negotiation leverage
  • Which fee add-ons apply to your orders

Restaurants that track effective cost per order instead of base commission make much better platform decisions.

Why Commission Comparison Matters

Most restaurant owners focus on headline commission rates, but profitability depends on the total cost per order.

Two restaurants paying the same 24% commission can have:

  • Completely different profit margins
  • Different ad costs
  • Different customer acquisition costs

Understanding the full comparison helps restaurants:

  • Optimise pricing
  • Negotiate better contracts
  • Improve margins

Base Commission Rates Compared

Neither platform publishes commission slabs. Swiggy describes its commission as based on terms "pre-agreed" with each restaurant (Swiggy Annual Report 2025-26), and Zomato sets it per restaurant in each partner's form (Zomato partner terms). Press reports put the market range at about 16–30%, with Swiggy specifically at 17–25% plus other charges.

Key Observations

  • Both platforms operate within similar commission bands.
  • Headline commission bands are similar, but fee add-ons differ: Swiggy charges a 2% collection fee (plus a Swiggy One fee for some partners), while Zomato adds a long-distance fee under a 30% overall cap.

Insight

At the base commission level there is little structural cost advantage between Swiggy and Zomato. The differences show up in the fees on top and in how you spend on ads and discounts.

Fees and GST Beyond Commission

The real cost difference emerges when additional charges are considered.

GST on Commission

Both platforms charge 18% GST on commission. Zomato's partner terms state that its fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.

Payment and Collection Fees

Payment processing is not included in commission on either platform.

  • Swiggy charges a 2% collection fee. It was introduced in December 2023 (Inc42) and extended to all orders in February 2025 (Free Press Journal).
  • Zomato charges a payment mechanism fee, a percentage of order value including taxes set in your contract (Zomato partner terms).

GST at 18% applies to these fees too.

Platform-Specific Fees

  • Swiggy One order fee: from 25 November 2025, Swiggy introduced a fee on Swiggy One orders for about 1% of its restaurant partners (Free Press Journal). Check whether it applies to your outlet.
  • Zomato long-distance fee: since mid-2025, Zomato charges restaurants an extra fee on longer-distance orders, while saying total charges are capped at 30% of order value (Outlook Business). Reported amounts differ, so confirm the current figure in your partner app.

Strategic Takeaways

  1. Base commission bands are nearly identical.
  2. Fee add-ons differ: collection and payment fees on both, a Swiggy One fee for some Swiggy partners, a long-distance fee on Zomato.
  3. Effective cost varies more by execution than by platform.
  4. Ad spend efficiency determines profitability.

  6. A dual-platform strategy usually maximises revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Swiggy or Zomato commission lower in 2026?

Base commission bands are almost the same. In the contracts we audit, both typically sit between 18% and 28%. Press reports put Swiggy at 17–25% plus other charges. The difference comes from fee add-ons, ads and discounts rather than the headline rate.

Do Swiggy and Zomato charge a payment fee on top of commission?

Yes. Swiggy charges a 2% collection fee and Zomato charges a payment mechanism fee set in your contract. GST at 18% applies to both. On a ₹500 order, a 2% fee plus GST is about ₹11.80.

What is the Swiggy One fee for restaurants?

From 25 November 2025, Swiggy introduced a fee on Swiggy One orders for about 1% of its restaurant partners. Check your agreement and payout statement to see whether it applies to you.

What is the effective take rate on Swiggy and Zomato?

Across PFC client accounts, commission, GST, payment fees and ads usually add up to about 25–40% of order value. In our illustrative ₹500 examples, that is about 36% on Swiggy and 39% on Zomato.

Can I get a lower commission by negotiating?

Often, through your official account manager, when you can show strong volume, ratings and outlet count. Savings depend on your contract and are not guaranteed.
