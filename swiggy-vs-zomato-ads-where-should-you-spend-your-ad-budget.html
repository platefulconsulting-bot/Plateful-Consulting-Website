Common mistakes to avoid

Investing only in one platform

This limits growth potential and leaves you without a comparison point.

Ignoring performance metrics

Without cost per order and conversion data, you cannot optimise.

Changing budgets too frequently

Constant changes prevent campaigns from learning and make results hard to read.

Promoting the entire menu

Spreading spend across every item reduces campaign efficiency. Push the items that already convert.

A five-step plan for allocating your ad budget

  1. Run test campaigns on both platforms.
  2. Track cost per order on each.
  3. Identify high-performing items and focus spend on them.
  4. Allocate a higher budget to the better-performing platform.
  5. Review performance monthly and rebalance.

Frequently asked questions

Which platform has cheaper ads, Swiggy or Zomato?

Neither is cheaper across the board. CPC varies with competition, location and cuisine category. In our audits Swiggy CPC tends to sit at a moderate level and Zomato at moderate to high, and press reports put Zomato sponsored listings at roughly ₹5 to ₹15 per click.

Can I run ads on both platforms at the same time?

Yes. Many restaurants use both for diversified reach and less dependence on a single platform. Start with a balanced split and adjust once you have cost-per-order data.

What ROAS should I target?

Most restaurants aim for at least 3x ROAS. In PFC client accounts we have seen baseline campaigns at 2x to 3x and optimised campaigns at 3x to 5x (illustrative; results vary and are not guaranteed).

Which platform is better for new restaurants?

Testing both is the safest route, because performance depends on your cuisine, city and pricing. For the testing phase, the daily budget we suggest in our audits is ₹200 to ₹300.

Should the budget be split equally?

An equal split is a sensible start. After that, move budget towards the platform with the lower cost per order and better conversion rate, and review monthly rather than daily.
