To start a cloud kitchen on Swiggy and Zomato, set up a delivery-only kitchen, get your FSSAI, GST and trade licences, build a delivery-friendly menu of 20–30 items, then register on both partner portals, clear verification and upload your menu. A basic setup in India typically takes 2–4 weeks and ₹2–8 lakh, depending on scale and city.

In short, the steps are:

  1. Set up a delivery-only kitchen space
  2. Obtain required licences (FSSAI, GST, Trade Licence)
  3. Design a delivery-optimised menu
  4. Register as a restaurant partner on both platforms
  5. Complete onboarding, menu upload, and go live

Why Cloud Kitchens Are Growing Rapidly

Cloud kitchens (delivery-only restaurants) have become one of the fastest-growing business models in India.

The reasons are structural:

  • No dine-in infrastructure cost
  • Lower rent requirements
  • High scalability
  • Direct access to demand via Swiggy and Zomato

For many operators, cloud kitchens can deliver higher ROI per square foot than dine-in restaurants, provided the unit economics are managed well.

Licensing Requirements (FSSAI, GST, Trade)

To operate legally on Swiggy and Zomato, you need the following:

1. FSSAI Licence (Mandatory)

  • Basic registration or State licence depending on scale
  • Required for all food businesses

2. GST Registration

  • Required for selling through aggregator platforms
  • Enables input tax credit

3. Trade Licence

  • Issued by the local municipal authority

4. Fire & Safety Compliance (if applicable)

  • Required for larger kitchen setups

5. Shop & Establishment Registration

  • Mandatory in most states

Important Note

Without proper documentation, platform onboarding will be rejected.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a cloud kitchen in India?

A basic cloud kitchen typically needs ₹2–3 lakh at low scale, ₹4–6 lakh at mid scale and ₹6–8 lakh for a premium setup. The main heads are kitchen equipment, rent deposit, licences, packaging and initial marketing.

Which licences do I need to list on Swiggy and Zomato?

You need an FSSAI licence or registration, GST registration and a trade licence from your local municipal authority. Shop & Establishment registration is mandatory in most states, and larger kitchens may also need fire and safety compliance. Without these, onboarding will be rejected.

How long does it take to go live on Swiggy and Zomato?

Usually 2–4 weeks in total: about 1–2 weeks for setup and licensing, 7–10 days for platform onboarding and 3–5 days for menu setup and photos.

How many items should a cloud kitchen menu have?

Keep it to around 20–30 items, focused on high-demand cuisines, combos and dishes that travel well. Avoid complex dishes, low-margin items and anything that loses quality during delivery.

What are the monthly running costs of a cloud kitchen?

Typical ranges are ₹15,000–₹60,000 for rent and ₹40,000–₹1,20,000 for staff, with raw materials at 30–40% of revenue and ads plus platform commissions at 25–40% of revenue. That is why cloud kitchens are low-capex but margin-sensitive.
