If your Swiggy or Zomato orders dropped suddenly, the cause is usually one of three things. Fewer customers are seeing your outlet (visibility), fewer of those who see it are ordering (conversion), or something outside your kitchen changed (demand, weather, platform rules, account issues). Check your partner dashboard funnel first, then fix the stage that broke.

I ran a bootstrapped cloud kitchen from 2019 to 2021, and I remember the panic of a dead Friday night. Since then, our team at Plateful Consulting has worked with 650+ restaurants. The pattern repeats. Owners change prices, offers and menus all at once, and then nobody knows what worked. This guide is a calmer way to diagnose the drop.

Have your orders dropped, or is it a normal swing?

Before you fix anything, confirm the drop is real. Delivery demand moves by weekday, by month-end salary cycles and by weather. A quiet Tuesday compared with last Saturday tells you nothing.

  • Compare like with like. This Tuesday against the last three Tuesdays. This week against the same week last month.
  • Check both platforms. If Swiggy and Zomato both fell on the same days, look at demand or your kitchen. If only one fell, look at that platform's listing first.
  • Look at the funnel, not just orders. Most partner dashboards break performance into impressions (how many people saw you), menu opens, carts and orders. Labels differ by app version, but the logic is the same. The stage where numbers fell tells you which group of causes to check.

The first 48 hours: a triage checklist

Do these in order. In our experience, most drops show up by step four or five. Change one thing at a time so you can see what worked.

  1. Pull day-wise orders for the last 30 days on both platforms and mark when the drop started.
  2. Check outlet status and online hours. Confirm the outlet was actually live during lunch and dinner peaks.
  3. Open the funnel and note which stage fell: impressions, menu opens or orders.
  4. Read every review and low rating from the past two weeks. Look for one repeated complaint.
  5. Check rejections, cancellations and kitchen prep time for the same period.
  6. List every change you made in the past three weeks: prices, offers, menu items, photos, timings, ads.
  7. Check which items are switched off as out of stock, especially your top sellers.
  8. Look at your nearest competitors on the customer app. Note new offers, ad slots or new outlets.
  9. Check your email and the partner app for compliance, document or payout notices.
  10. If something still looks wrong on the platform's side, raise a support ticket with screenshots and dates.

Visibility causes: fewer people see your outlet

Ranking on both apps depends on how reliably you serve customers. We explain this in detail in our guides to the Swiggy ranking algorithm and Zomato restaurant ranking. Here are the six visibility causes we see most often.

1. Rating dip

Signal: rating down even by a small amount, with a cluster of recent low ratings. Fix: find the repeated complaint (cold food, small portion, wrong item, spill) and fix that one thing. Reply to reviews politely. Our Swiggy rating recovery guide covers the full process.

2. Availability and offline time

Signal: online hours below your listed timings, or the outlet going offline during peaks. Tablets that log out, phones on silent and staff switching off the outlet when busy are common reasons. Fix: keep the outlet live through every listed hour. Zomato's own online ordering terms ask partners to switch off accepting orders only when they genuinely cannot serve, and to remove unavailable items.

3. Kitchen prep time and rejections

Signal: prep time creeping up, more rejections, or more "restaurant delay" complaints. Fix: prep the top ten items in advance, fix staffing at peaks and set a realistic prep time. Do not accept orders you cannot cook. A rejected order hurts more than a slower one.

4. Menu and photo issues

Signal: menu opens are fine but item clicks are low, or bestsellers were edited recently. Fix: check that top items still have clear photos, honest descriptions and correct prices. A recent menu edit can also reset what customers see first.

5. A competitor's ads or offers

Signal: impressions down while nothing changed on your side, and a nearby outlet is now showing in ad slots or with a bigger offer. Fix: do not copy their discount blindly. Compare your price-to-portion value, test a small, targeted ad budget and protect your rating.

6. New-outlet boost ending

Signal: strong orders in the first weeks after launch, then a steady slide with no other change. Many owners see their best weeks right after launch. Fix: treat the launch period as a test. Use it to build ratings and repeat customers, so you hold your position once the early push ends.

Conversion causes: people see you but do not order

7. Price or discount changes

Signal: menu-to-order conversion fell right after you raised prices or ended an offer. Fix: roll back the change on your top two or three items and watch for a week. Raise prices item by item, not all at once. Our guide on which Swiggy and Zomato offers make money helps you choose offers you can afford.

8. Out-of-stock items

Signal: bestsellers switched off for hours, or a customer complaint about "item not available" after ordering. Fix: plan stock for your top sellers first. Switch items back on as soon as stock arrives. Make one person responsible for this daily.

9. Poor recent reviews

Signal: new reviews mention the same problem, and they now sit at the top of your listing. Fix: fix the root cause before chasing new reviews. Packaging is often the hidden problem in delivery. Then encourage happy repeat customers to leave honest feedback.

Platform and external causes: the market moved

10. City demand, weather and festivals

Signal: both platforms fell on the same days, and neighbours report the same thing. Weather matters more than most owners expect. In Eternal's Q2 FY26 results, Zomato's leadership said extreme heat and extended rains were weighing on near-term growth, as reported by Moneycontrol. Festivals such as Navratri also change what people order. Fix: plan festival menus, keep the outlet live in rain and do not cut prices in panic during a city-wide dip.

11. Policy or fee changes

Signal: orders steady but payouts down, or a new line on your invoice. For example, Swiggy announced an extra per-order fee for select partners linked to the share of orders from SwiggyOne members, reported by Angel One in November 2025. The same report put commissions at 17% to 25%, but your rate depends on your contract. Fix: read your contract and statement line by line. Our guide on reading your payout statement shows where each deduction sits.

12. Account issues: duplicate listings and compliance holds

Signal: one platform drops sharply while the other holds, or the outlet disappears from search. Expired documents are a real risk. In July 2026, the Cyberabad Municipal Corporation directed Swiggy and Zomato to remove outlets with expired, suspended or cancelled licences. Fix: keep FSSAI and trade licences current, check for duplicate listings at your address, and raise a ticket with proof.

Why did my Swiggy orders drop while Zomato held steady?

When only one platform falls, start with that platform's listing, not your food. In our Burrito Bros case study in Pune, Swiggy sales dipped mid-plan while Zomato kept growing. The cause was a duplicate-outlet visibility issue on Swiggy's side. It was resolved with Swiggy, and Swiggy sales recovered. Changing prices or offers at that point would only have muddied the data.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my Zomato orders dropping suddenly?

A sudden Zomato drop usually comes from lower visibility, lower conversion or an outside change. Check whether your outlet was online during peaks, whether your rating slipped, and whether rejections or prep time went up. Then compare menu-to-order conversion before and after any price or offer change. If Swiggy stayed steady in the same period, look closely at your Zomato listing and account.

Why is my cloud kitchen not getting orders?

A cloud kitchen with few orders is often under-seen rather than disliked. New outlets need ratings, clear photos, fair prices and steady online hours to build visibility. Check your impressions first. If impressions are low, work on availability, ratings and listing quality. If impressions are fine but orders are low, fix your menu, pricing and offers.

Why does Zomato show that my restaurant is not accepting orders?

This usually means the outlet is switched off, outside its listed timings, or has been taken offline automatically. Zomato's partner terms allow it to cancel an order, and in some cases temporarily switch off access, when a restaurant does not respond in time. Check the partner app status, tablet login and outlet timings. If everything looks correct and the issue continues, raise a support ticket with screenshots.

Should I increase discounts when my orders drop?

Not as a first step. A bigger discount can bring orders back briefly but often hides the real cause and cuts your margin. First find which funnel stage fell. If conversion dropped after a price rise, adjust prices on key items. If visibility dropped, fix availability, ratings and listing quality. Use offers as a planned test with a fixed budget and end date.

When should I contact Swiggy or Zomato support about a drop?

Contact support when the drop affects only one platform, your outlet does not appear in search, your listing shows wrong details, or you suspect a duplicate listing at your address. Collect day-wise order data, screenshots and the date the drop began before raising the ticket. Clear evidence makes the ticket easier to act on. For drops on both platforms, check your own operations first.
