Example

If you spend ₹1,000 on ads and generate ₹5,000 in revenue, ROAS = 5x.

In our client work we set a minimum target of 3x ROAS, and some high-performing listings reach 5x or more. These are targets, not guarantees, and the right minimum for you depends on your margins.

Why a 3x ROAS can still lose money

ROAS here is based on revenue, not profit. Once commission, GST on it, food and packaging come out, a "good-looking" ROAS can leave nothing. Illustrative maths for ₹1,000 of ad spend at 3x ROAS, with 25% commission, 32% food cost and 5% packaging:

LineAmount
Revenue from ads (3x)₹3,000
Commission at 25% plus 18% GST on it−₹885
Food cost (32%) and packaging (5%)−₹1,110
Ad spend−₹1,000
Left over₹5

That is break-even before any discount, other platform fees or GST on the ad invoice. Add a restaurant-funded discount and the same campaign is in loss. At 30% commission, break-even ROAS rises to about 3.6x. Work out your own break-even ROAS (1 ÷ the share of revenue left after commission, GST, food and packaging) before you judge any campaign. Figures are illustrative; your results will vary.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5x ROAS realistic on Swiggy Ads?

Some well-run listings reach 5x or more in our client accounts, but it is a target, not a promise. Results depend on your rating, menu, category, city and competition, and are not guaranteed.

Are Swiggy ads charged per click?

Most listing ads we run for clients are billed per click, but billing can differ by ad format. Check the billing basis shown in your Partner app before you start a campaign.

What is a good ROAS on Swiggy?

One that is above your own break-even. With 25% commission plus GST, 32% food cost and 5% packaging, break-even is about 3x revenue ROAS; at 30% commission it is about 3.6x. Work out your number before judging any campaign.

How much should I spend on Swiggy ads per day?

We usually start clients at ₹200–₹400 a day for the first two weeks, then move budget to the items and time slots with the lowest cost per order. Scale only campaigns that are profitable after commission.

How do I check what Swiggy is charging me for ads?

Compare the ads deduction on your weekly payout statement with the spend shown in your ads dashboard. Raise any mismatch with your account manager in writing.
