The Swiggy partner dashboard and the Zomato restaurant partner app are where you run your delivery business day to day: accept orders, switch items and the outlet on or off, set timings, run offers and ads, read reviews and check payouts. Log in only through partner.swiggy.com, zomato.com/partners/onlineordering or the official apps, and check the same ten screens every week.

This guide is for owners and managers who already have a live listing, or are about to get one. It covers what each screen is for, what to check weekly, and what to do when orders stop coming in. Feature names change with app updates, so where we could not confirm an exact label from an official source, we describe the screen in plain words.

Both platforms give you a mobile app and a browser version. Use the login details registered during onboarding. Which screens in the Swiggy partner app actually matter?

The Swiggy Partner App's store description lists order management, menu management, complaints, ratings, business insights and finances, discounts and ads. In practice, most owners need six areas.

  • Orders. Receive and manage incoming orders for your outlet. Keep the order device on a charger with notifications and volume on so nothing is missed.
  • Menu and stock. Edit items, prices, photos and add-ons, and switch individual items off when you run out.
  • Outlet status and timings. Turn the outlet on or off and set daily opening hours. A wrong timing here is one of the most common reasons for silent days.
  • Discounts and ads. Create and pause offers and ad campaigns, and see what each one spent and returned.
  • Ratings, reviews and complaints. Customer ratings and complaints, gathered in one place.
  • Finances and insights. Payout details, invoices and business insights such as item-level sales and revenue trends, across outlets.

Two newer tools sit on top of these. In July 2024 Swiggy launched a Market Intelligence Dashboard that compares your outlet with similar restaurants on business, operations, funnel and spend metrics. In August 2026 it added Guru, an AI assistant inside the restaurant partner app. Swiggy says Guru can show payout summaries and tax reports, start or change ad and discount campaigns, analyse sales and orders, and add menu items, in over 20 Indian languages. Treat any campaign it creates like one you created yourself: check the budget and dates before it goes live.

Which screens in the Zomato restaurant partner dashboard matter?

Zomato's own App Store listing for Zomato Restaurant Partner names the main sections: menu management (items, categories, sub-categories and variants), live order management, inventory or stock tracking, business reporting, ads and promotions, and multi-outlet access. The browser version of the Zomato restaurant partner dashboard mirrors most of this and is easier for reports and downloads.

  • Orders. Accept and track live orders. Keep the order device on a charger with volume up.
  • Menu. Edit items and variants, and toggle availability.
  • Outlet timings and status. Daily hours and temporary closures.
  • Business reporting. Daily trends in sales, orders and related metrics.
  • Ads and promotions. Campaigns, discounts and their results.
  • Reviews and complaints. Ratings and order issues you can respond to.
  • Payouts and invoices. Settlement details and monthly invoices.
  • Help. Use the Help or Support section in the app. Zomato's online ordering terms point partners to the help centre on the merchant app for promotion-related queries. For anything that stops you fulfilling orders, the terms say to contact your Zomato account manager or email priority@zomato.com.

The Zomato partner app has also offered a Restaurant Services Hub for paid services like hiring and licence paperwork, as Business Today reported in June 2024. It is optional and separate from your order operations.

What should you check in the partner dashboard every week?

Block 30 minutes every Monday. Open both dashboards side by side and go through this list.

  1. Net sales and orders vs last week. Look at the trend, not one day. A drop that lasts more than a few days needs a cause.
  2. Outlet availability. How many hours you were actually online vs your listed hours. Unplanned offline time hurts visibility.
  3. Timings for the coming week. Fix any festival, holiday or staff-leave changes in advance.
  4. Out-of-stock items. Switch back on anything you restocked. Items left off for days quietly shrink your menu.
  5. Ratings and new reviews. Read every review under four stars and note repeat issues like packaging, quantity or wrong items.
  6. Complaints, cancellations and refunds. Check which ones were charged to you and why. Our guide on cancellations, refunds and penalties explains who pays.
  7. Preparation time and handover. Slow preparation or late handover makes delivery partners wait and can hurt your ratings.
  8. Ad spend and return. Orders and sales from each campaign vs what it cost. Pause what is not paying back.
  9. Discount cost. How much of your gross sales went into offers. See which offers actually make money.
  10. Payout statement. Match the payout to your sales, commission, ads, taxes and deductions. Our guide on how to read your payout statement walks through each line.

How do you add staff or manage multiple outlets?

Do not share the owner login with the whole kitchen. If a staff member leaves, you lose control of who can change prices or run ads.

  • Zomato. The partner app supports multi-outlet access and switching between outlets from one login. Look in settings for a staff or team section to add users for outlet operations. If you cannot see it, raise it from the Help or Support section or with your account manager.
  • Swiggy. The partner app shows business insights across outlets, and multi-outlet owners can usually see linked outlets from one login. For adding a manager or a new outlet to your account, use the Help or Support section or your account manager, since access is tied to registered contact details.
  • New outlet, new listing. A second location is normally onboarded as its own outlet, with its own FSSAI licence details and address, then linked to your account.

Keep a simple register: who has access, on which phone number, and what they are allowed to change. Remove access the day someone leaves.

Frequently asked questions

What is the official Swiggy partner dashboard login?

The official browser login is partner.swiggy.com, and the mobile app is the Swiggy Partner App published by Swiggy in your phone's official app store. Log in with the details registered during onboarding. Avoid links sent on WhatsApp or SMS by people claiming to be Swiggy staff. Type the address yourself so you do not hand your login to a fake page.

Is the Zomato restaurant partner app free to use?

The Zomato Restaurant Partner app is listed as a free download on the App Store. You pay commission and any ad or offer costs under your partner contract, not for the app itself. Paid services offered through the app, such as vendors in the Restaurant Services Hub, are optional and priced separately by the provider.

Can I manage several outlets from one partner app?

Yes. Zomato's partner app lists multi-outlet access with an outlet switcher, and Swiggy's partner app shows insights across outlets, with linked outlets usually visible from one login. Each outlet is still a separate listing with its own address, licence details, timings and menu. Check timings and stock per outlet, because one outlet left offline can go unnoticed in combined numbers.

What is Swiggy Guru in the partner app?

Guru is an AI assistant Swiggy added to its restaurant partner app in August 2026. Swiggy says it can summarise payouts and tax reports, start or edit ad and discount campaigns, analyse sales and orders, and help add menu items, in over 20 Indian languages. It speeds up routine tasks, but review any campaign budget it sets before approving.

Why does my partner app show orders late or not ring?

Usually the phone, not the platform. Check that notifications are allowed for the partner app, volume is up, Do Not Disturb is off, and battery saver is not closing the app in the background. Use one dedicated device that stays on charge with a stable internet connection. If alerts still fail after an app update, raise a ticket from the app's Help or Support section.
