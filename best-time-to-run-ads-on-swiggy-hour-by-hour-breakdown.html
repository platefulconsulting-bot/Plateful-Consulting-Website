Why Timing Matters More Than Budget

Many restaurants assume that increasing ad budget improves results.

However, ads shown during low-demand hours often generate:

  • lower conversion rate
  • higher cost per order
  • inefficient spend

Demand concentration varies through the day. Aligning ad spend with peak order windows improves return on spend.

Frequently asked questions

Should I run Swiggy ads all day?

Usually not. Most restaurants get better returns by concentrating budget in peak ordering hours, mainly lunch (11:30 AM to 1:30 PM) and dinner (7:00 PM to 9:30 PM), rather than spreading it across 24 hours.

Which slot has the best conversion?

Dinner hours typically produce the highest conversion rates and order volume, which is why a 40 to 50% share of the daily ad budget is a sensible starting point for dinner.

Do Swiggy ads perform differently by cuisine?

Yes. Cuisine strongly influences demand timing. North Indian and biryani do well at lunch and dinner, while pizza, burgers and desserts lean towards evening and late night.

Are weekend ads more effective?

Weekends often bring higher dinner orders, more group ordering and a higher average order value, so weekend campaigns may justify a larger budget.

How often should I optimise ad timing?

Review timing performance every 2 to 4 weeks, and make smaller budget shifts weekly based on cost per order by slot.
