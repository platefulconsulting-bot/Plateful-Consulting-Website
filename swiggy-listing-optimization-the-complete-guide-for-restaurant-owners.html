Cuisine tags that match search intent

Cuisine tags determine which search results your restaurant appears in. Incorrect tagging reduces visibility among relevant customers.

Common tagging mistakes

  • selecting too many categories
  • choosing irrelevant cuisines
  • ignoring high-search categories
  • failing to include your primary cuisine

Example

A restaurant serving butter chicken, paneer tikka and dal makhani should use tags such as:

  • North Indian
  • Mughlai
  • Biryani (if applicable)

It should avoid unrelated tags like Italian or Chinese.

Best practice

Choose 3–5 highly relevant tags aligned with your core menu. Relevant tags increase how often you appear in:

  • search results
  • filtered categories
  • recommendation sections

Menu layout optimization

Menu structure influences how quickly customers decide what to order. A cluttered menu reduces conversion.

Recommended menu layout

  1. Best sellers
  2. Combos
  3. Main items
  4. Sides
  5. Beverages

Ideal item count

Most high-performing listings maintain around 20–30 items. Too many options increase decision fatigue; too few reduce choice confidence.

Minimum order value (MOV) strategy

Minimum order value affects customer decision-making. If MOV is too high, customers may abandon checkout. If it is too low, order profitability suffers.

Recommended MOV range

Roughly ₹150–₹300, depending on category.

CategoryTypical MOV
Fast foodLower MOV
Casual diningMedium MOV
Premium cuisineHigher MOV

MOV should align with the average order value customers expect in your category.

Operating hours optimization

Operating hours influence ranking and availability. Frequent closures reduce listing visibility.

Best practices

  • maintain consistent opening hours
  • avoid frequent manual closures
  • ensure staff readiness during peak hours
  • avoid rejecting orders due to stock issues

Listings with a stable operating history tend to perform better in ranking.

Availability and order acceptance rate

Swiggy tracks operational reliability. Negative signals include:

  • rejecting orders
  • going offline frequently
  • inconsistent preparation time

These signals reduce algorithm trust in your listing.

Fix

Maintain:

  • consistent kitchen readiness
  • accurate preparation times
  • proper inventory planning

Consistency improves long-term ranking stability.

Frequently asked questions

How many cuisine tags should I use on Swiggy?

Choose 3–5 highly relevant tags that match your core menu, including your primary cuisine. Avoid unrelated tags, as incorrect tagging reduces visibility among the customers most likely to order.

How many items should a Swiggy menu have?

Most high-performing listings maintain around 20–30 items, arranged as best sellers, combos, main items, sides and beverages. Too many options cause decision fatigue, while too few reduce choice confidence.

What minimum order value should I set on Swiggy?

A range of roughly ₹150–₹300 works for many restaurants, depending on category: lower for fast food, medium for casual dining and higher for premium cuisine. Align it with the average order value customers expect.

Does order rejection affect Swiggy ranking?

Yes. Rejecting orders, going offline frequently and inconsistent preparation times are negative reliability signals. Consistent kitchen readiness, accurate preparation times and proper inventory planning help ranking stability.

How long does Swiggy listing optimization take to show results?

A practical plan runs over about four weeks: photos, tags and menu in week 1, MOV and descriptions in week 2, and item and combo clean-up in weeks 3–4. Many listings show early movement within 2–4 weeks, but results vary and are not guaranteed.
