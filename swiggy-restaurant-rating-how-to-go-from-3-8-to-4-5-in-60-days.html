A Swiggy rating can move from around 3.8 towards 4.5 in about 60 days, but only if the restaurant fixes the causes behind low ratings: packaging, order accuracy, kitchen consistency, review responses and preparation-time reliability. The pace depends on order volume and how severe the issues are, so no timeline or final rating is guaranteed.

In practice, restaurants can work on four operational areas:

  • packaging quality and presentation
  • order accuracy and kitchen consistency
  • response strategy for negative reviews
  • preparation time reliability

Ratings improve when the customer experience becomes predictable and consistent. Restaurants that put structured SOP improvements in place often see their rating start to improve within roughly 3–8 weeks, depending on order volume and issue severity (illustrative; results vary and are not guaranteed).

Ratings strongly influence how customers decide where to order.

Customers tend to see ratings below 4.0 as risky. A better rating helps both trust and ranking.

Packaging Fixes That Stop Negative Reviews

Packaging issues often create strong negative impressions, and customers frequently link packaging problems with poor food quality.

Packaging Improvements

  • leak-proof containers for gravies
  • ventilated packaging for fried items
  • insulated packaging for heat retention
  • tamper-evident seals

Better packaging reduces how often the same complaints come up, and it is often the first change whose effect shows in reviews within a few weeks.

Kitchen SOP for Consistency

Consistency is critical for a positive customer experience. Variations in taste, portion or presentation lead to negative reviews.

Kitchen SOP Elements

  • standardised recipes
  • fixed portion measurements
  • preparation time guidelines
  • order accuracy checklist

A structured SOP reduces variability, and consistency makes repeat orders more likely.

Order Accuracy Control

Incorrect orders are one of the most common causes of low ratings. Examples include:

  • missing items
  • incorrect variants
  • incomplete combos

Accurate orders build trust.

Order Verification Checklist

Before dispatch:

  • confirm item count
  • verify variants
  • check packaging seal
  • confirm add-ons

A simple verification step reduces avoidable errors.

Review Response Strategy

Customers often judge a restaurant by how it responds to feedback, so professional responses improve brand perception.

Best Practices for Responding to Reviews

  • acknowledge the issue politely
  • avoid a defensive tone
  • offer a resolution where possible
  • keep a consistent communication style

Response quality affects how customers see your brand.

Example Response Structure

  • thank the customer
  • acknowledge the concern
  • assure improvement
  • keep a professional tone

Consistent responses strengthen trust.

Preparation Time Reliability

Incorrect preparation time settings create customer dissatisfaction, and late orders often lead to negative reviews.

Optimisation Methods

  • set a realistic preparation time
  • avoid accepting orders beyond kitchen capacity
  • align kitchen workflow with peak demand

Reliable timing improves customer satisfaction.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Swiggy rating?

Ratings above 4.2 are generally considered strong for maintaining high visibility. Below 4.0, customers tend to see a listing as risky and click-through and conversion usually suffer.

Can I remove fake negative reviews on Swiggy?

Platforms may review disputed feedback, but removal is not guaranteed. The more dependable route is to fix the operational causes of complaints so that new, positive reviews outweigh old ones.

How long does it take to recover a dropped rating?

Many restaurants see measurable improvement within 4–8 weeks of operational changes, faster for higher-volume outlets (illustrative; results vary and are not guaranteed).

Does packaging affect ratings significantly?

Yes. Leakage, damage and cold food frequently appear in negative reviews, and customers often link packaging problems with poor food quality.

Should I respond to all reviews?

Responding professionally improves brand perception. Thank the customer, acknowledge the concern, assure improvement and avoid a defensive tone.
