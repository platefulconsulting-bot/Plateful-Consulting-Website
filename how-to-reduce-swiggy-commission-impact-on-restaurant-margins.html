Restaurants reduce the impact of Swiggy commission by building their unit economics around it rather than chasing a lower rate. The working levers are delivery menu pricing, average order value (AOV), food cost percentage, combos and controlled discounting. Most outlets cannot remove commission, but every order can still be designed to leave a healthy contribution margin.

For context, Swiggy typically charges 18–27% and Zomato 18–28% base commission in contracts PFC audits; press reports put the range at roughly 16–30%. The levers to protect margin against that cost are:

  • menu pricing structure
  • average order value (AOV)
  • food cost percentage
  • combo strategy
  • discount dependency

In PFC client accounts we have seen well-structured menu engineering improve profit per order by roughly 8–20%, even with platform commission applied (illustrative; results vary and are not guaranteed).

5 Ways to Offset Commission Impact

None of these depend on a special rate from the platform. They are legitimate levers any restaurant can work on.

1. Increase Average Order Value (AOV)

Higher order value improves contribution margin per transaction, because fixed costs like packaging are spread over a bigger bill.

Methods to Increase AOV

  • combo meals
  • add-ons
  • meal upgrades
  • bundle pricing

Illustrative example: moving a typical order from ₹250 to ₹380 gives a much larger margin buffer after commission.

2. Optimise Menu Pricing

Delivery pricing should reflect commission impact. Many restaurants keep delivery prices about 10–20% higher than dine-in prices.

Balanced pricing protects margins without hurting conversion.

3. Reduce Food Cost Percentage

Food cost optimisation gives you more room to absorb commission.

Target food cost range in our audits: 28–35%.

Menu engineering helps you identify high-margin items and push them.

4. Control Discount Dependency

Excessive discounting sharply reduces contribution margin, especially when it sits on top of commission. Controlled promotions improve long-term profitability.

Alternatives include:

  • combo pricing
  • minimum order discounts
  • add-on offers

5. Optimise Menu Mix

Menu mix influences profitability. High-performing menus typically include:

  • high-margin items
  • scalable recipes
  • ingredient overlap

Ingredient overlap reduces inventory complexity and wastage.

Negotiating Swiggy Commission

Commission rates may vary depending on:

  • order volume
  • brand recognition
  • city competition level

A review of your contract terms may be possible in certain situations, for example when your volumes have grown. Avoid deals that tie a lower rate to listing on only one platform. Inc42, citing Reuters, reported in November 2024 that the Competition Commission of India's investigation found Zomato had entered into exclusivity contracts with restaurant partners in return for lower commissions.

In our experience, most restaurants gain more from improving unit economics than from focusing only on the commission percentage.

Commission vs Volume Trade-Off

Higher order volume does not automatically improve profitability. Profitability depends on contribution margin per order, so growing volume on loss-making orders only grows the loss. A balanced approach is more sustainable.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Swiggy take?

Swiggy typically charges 18–27% and Zomato 18–28% base commission in contracts PFC audits; press reports put the range at roughly 16–30%. GST on commission, payment gateway charges and optional promotions come on top, depending on your agreement.

Can I negotiate Swiggy commission?

A review may be possible depending on order volume, brand positioning and city competition. Do not accept a lower rate in exchange for listing exclusively on one platform. Most restaurants gain more from fixing pricing, AOV and food cost.

Is Swiggy commission charged on total bill or food value?

Commission is typically applied to order value, based on the terms of your contract. Check the merchant terms in your own agreement to see exactly what the percentage is charged on.

How can I maintain profit despite commission?

Raise AOV with combos and add-ons, price the delivery menu for platform costs, keep food cost around 28–35%, and limit discount dependency. Calculate contribution margin per dish so you know which items actually make money.

Should I price delivery higher than dine-in?

Many restaurants keep delivery menu prices about 10–20% higher than dine-in to account for commission and packaging. Test the difference against your conversion rate so pricing does not hurt orders.
