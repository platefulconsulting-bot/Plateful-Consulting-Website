Zomato commission in 2026 is not one fixed number. Reported base rates run from about 16% to 30% of order value, and your rate depends on your contract, city, volume and brand. On top sit 18% GST on the commission, a payment fee of around 2% and a long-distance fee on farther orders.

Quick answer

  • Base commission: reported at around 16–30% for the large delivery apps (Outlook Business, June 2025). Bengaluru hoteliers cited 8% to 28% in July 2026. Your exact rate is in your enrolment form.
  • GST on commission: 18%. Most restaurants pay 5% GST on food without input tax credit, so this 18% cannot be offset. It is a real cost.
  • Payment mechanism fee: a percentage of order value including taxes, around 2% in reported cases, plus GST.
  • Long-distance fee: reported figures differ. A June 2025 Moneycontrol report, carried by Outlook Business, put it at ₹20 for 4–6 km on orders above ₹150 and ₹40 beyond 6 km. Inc42 (July 2025) reported ₹15 and ₹25–35. Check your payout statement for your figure.
  • Other costs: ads, restaurant-funded discounts and packaging. TDS of 0.1% is a tax credit you can claim, not a cost.
  • Typical total: in our worked example below, commission plus GST and payment fee takes about 31% of a ₹400 order, before ads or discounts.

How does Zomato commission work in 2026?

Zomato does not publish a public rate card. Each restaurant signs an enrolment form that sets its rates. The online ordering terms on Eternal's site call the main charge a "service fee", formerly known as commission. They also define a payment mechanism fee as a share of order value including taxes.

Zomato deducts these amounts before it sends your payout. The terms also allow Zomato to revise fees after giving you notice. So the rate you signed two years ago may not be the rate you pay today. Read every fee notice in your partner app.

Commission is charged on revenue, not profit. A 24% commission on a dish with a 35% food cost does not leave 41% as profit. That 41% still has to pay for GST on fees, packaging, rent, staff and your own salary. This is why a three-point gap in commission can change the year for a small outlet.

A note on the 30% cap. Zomato reportedly caps what it charges at 30% of order value. In this example, commission plus distance fee is ₹116, or 29%, which stays under 30%. The reported cap appears to apply to commission plus distance fee, not to GST or the payment fee. That is why total deductions can cross 30%. Zomato has not published how the cap is applied, so check your own statement.

GST may also apply to the distance fee, depending on how it is billed to you. Now subtract food cost. At 35% food cost (₹140), the within-4-km order leaves about ₹137 for packaging, rent, staff and profit. Run a restaurant-funded discount or ads on top, and that number shrinks fast.

Now scale it. At 1,000 orders a month of ₹400 each, a 24% commission versus 21% is ₹12,000 a month in commission. Add 18% GST and the gap is ₹14,160 a month, or about ₹1.7 lakh a year. That is the value of reviewing your rate with real data. Test your own volume in the commission calculator.

Why the 18% GST on Zomato commission costs more than it looks

A business that charges 18% GST on its own sales can usually claim back the GST it pays on services. Most restaurants cannot. Restaurant food is taxed at 5% without input tax credit, whether sold directly or through apps, as the Minister of State for Finance confirmed in April 2025. So the 18% on commission is a straight cost.

In practice, a 24% commission behaves like about 28.3% once GST is added. Always compare contracts on the after-GST number.

What changed for Zomato restaurants in 2025–2026?

  • Long-distance fee (mid-2025): a per-order fee on deliveries beyond 4 km. Reported amounts differ between outlets, and restaurant bodies raised concerns. Check it against your own distance mix.
  • Price parity: Zomato says it removed price-parity requirements earlier in 2026 and does not include exclusivity clauses in standard agreements. The NRAI case at the CCI is still running (Business Standard, July 2026).
  • Consent for promotions (August 2026): after talks with Bengaluru hotel bodies, Zomato agreed that promotions will be opt-in, with OTP approval before a promotion starts, as reported by Angel One in August 2026.

How to reduce Zomato commission impact legitimately

You cannot make the commission vanish. You can pay less of it per rupee of profit. This checklist uses only legit levers: no fake reviews, no hidden rates, no side deals.

  1. Find your real rate. Pull your enrolment form and the last three payout statements. Write down commission, payment fee and distance fees per order.
  2. Calculate your after-GST take. Multiply your commission by 1.18. That is the number to manage.
  3. Check your distance mix. If many orders go beyond 4 km, compare your delivery radius against what those orders really earn.
  4. Price for delivery. Build delivery prices that absorb commission and packaging, instead of copying the dine-in menu.
  5. Raise order value. Combos and add-ons spread fixed costs, like the distance fee, over a bigger bill.
  6. Control discounts. Promotions are now opt-in. Approve only offers that still make money after commission.
  7. Improve order quality. Fewer rejections, faster prep and better ratings build your case at renewal.
  8. Review the contract every 6–12 months. Go in with volume and quality data. Outcomes vary by account.

Frequently asked questions

What is Zomato commission for cloud kitchens?

Zomato does not publish a separate cloud kitchen rate. Cloud kitchens sign the same type of enrolment form, and the rate depends on volume, brand and city, within the reported band of roughly 16–30%. The GST Council treats cloud kitchens as restaurant service, so 5% GST without input tax credit applies. Because cloud kitchens rely fully on delivery, distance fees hit harder.

What is the Zomato commission percentage in 2026?

There is no single public percentage. Reported ranges run from about 16% to 30% of order value, and one Bengaluru association cited 8% to 28% in July 2026. Your rate is in your enrolment form. Add 18% GST, so a 24% commission costs about 28.3% in practice, before payment and distance fees.

Is GST charged on Zomato commission?

Yes. Zomato adds 18% GST on its commission and other fees. Most restaurants pay 5% GST on food without input tax credit, so they cannot claim this GST back. Treat it as part of your commission cost. The 5% GST on food is charged to the customer, and since 1 January 2022 the app pays it to the government for app orders.
