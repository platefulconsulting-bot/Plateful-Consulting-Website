Why Most Restaurants Struggle to Grow Zomato Orders

Many restaurant owners assume low Zomato orders are caused by platform bias or high competition. In our audits, most underperforming listings fail on three key signals:

  • Low click-through rate from search results
  • Low conversion rate after customers open the menu
  • Low repeat orders due to an inconsistent experience

The Zomato marketplace works much like a search engine. The platform keeps observing which restaurants customers click, order from and rate highly. Restaurants that perform well on these signals get shown more often.

The implication is important: visibility on Zomato is earned through performance signals, not simply ads.

Ads can accelerate growth, but without the underlying signals (strong photos, competitive pricing, reliable delivery) ads alone cannot sustain order growth.

Listing Optimization Checklist

The fastest way to increase Zomato orders is to optimise the restaurant listing itself. In our audits, most listings are incomplete or poorly structured.

Below is a practical checklist we use in restaurant revenue audits.

1. Professional Food Photography

In our audits, listings with good food photos consistently get more clicks and menu opens than listings without them.

Best practices:

  • Use top-down food shots
  • Avoid cluttered backgrounds
  • Make sure the lighting shows off food texture
  • Add photos to at least the top 70% of menu items

Zomato search results are visual. If your listing lacks strong food photography, customers may never even open your menu.

2. Optimise Cuisine Tags

Cuisine tags determine where your restaurant appears in Zomato search.

Example: if your restaurant sells biryani and kebabs, the correct tags may include:

  • Biryani
  • North Indian
  • Mughlai

Incorrect tags can suppress visibility in the categories where customers actually search. Restaurants frequently mis-tag themselves, which leaves them hidden despite good ratings.

3. Delivery-Optimised Menu Structure

Your delivery menu should not copy your dine-in menu.

Successful Zomato delivery menus typically include:

  • 20–30 items maximum
  • High-margin dishes
  • Combo bundles
  • Clear category organisation

A shorter menu speeds up the customer's decision, which directly improves order conversion.

Ad performance depends heavily on listing quality. Ads that send traffic to a weak listing produce poor returns, because you pay for the click whether or not the customer orders.

Restaurants should measure cost per order, not impressions.

Operational Consistency: The Hidden Ranking Factor

Many restaurants underestimate how strongly operational metrics influence Zomato visibility.

Key operational signals include:

  • Order acceptance rate
  • Preparation time accuracy
  • Order cancellation rate
  • Restaurant availability hours

Listings that frequently reject orders or close unexpectedly lose visibility and customer trust, a point trade reporting on platform penalties also makes.

Operational reliability keeps the restaurant appearing in search results.

Can Restaurants Grow Zomato Orders Without Ads?

Yes, but it requires stronger organic signals.

Restaurants that grow orders organically typically focus on:

  • Photo-rich menus
  • Competitive pricing
  • High ratings
  • Strong combo offers

Organic growth is slower at first but tends to be more profitable over time because ad costs stay low.

In our experience, many high-performing restaurants reduce ad spend once they have strong ranking momentum.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to increase Zomato orders?

Start with the listing itself: add good food photos to most menu items, correct your cuisine tags and check that pricing is competitive with similar restaurants. In our audits, most listings are incomplete, so this is usually where the quickest gains sit.

Do Zomato ads guarantee more orders?

No. Sponsored listings are charged per click and Zomato's terms say it does not guarantee a minimum number of clicks. Ads work best when they send traffic to a listing that already converts well, so track cost per order rather than impressions.

What rating should a restaurant aim for on Zomato?

In our audits, listings below 4.0 lose visibility, and restaurants that hold 4.3 or higher tend to rank better. Restaurants can improve ratings by fixing the operational causes of bad reviews, such as cold food, spills, wrong orders and slow prep.

How many items should a Zomato delivery menu have?

Successful delivery menus typically have 20–30 items, organised into clear categories with best sellers, combos, add-ons and a few premium dishes. A shorter menu helps customers decide faster.

Can a restaurant grow on Zomato without running ads?

Yes, with strong organic signals: photo-rich menus, competitive pricing, high ratings and good combo offers. Growth is slower at first but usually more profitable because ad costs stay low.
