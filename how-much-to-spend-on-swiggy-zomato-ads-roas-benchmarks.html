What ad types do Swiggy and Zomato offer restaurants?

Both platforms sell paid visibility inside their apps, but they document it very differently. Zomato publishes its advertising terms. Swiggy explains its tools mostly through the partner app and announcements.

On Zomato, the core product is the Sponsored Listing Service. Its published terms on Eternal's policy site define the fee as cost per click. A click counts when a customer reaches your restaurant page by tapping the ad, or within 24 hours of last viewing it. Fees are capped at the campaign budget agreed for the start and end dates and are deducted from your weekly payouts. The terms also say Zomato does not guarantee a minimum number of clicks or any result.

How much should your Swiggy Zomato ads budget be?

There is no correct rupee figure for every outlet. A dhaba doing lakhs a month and a new cloud kitchen with 10 orders a day need different budgets. What every outlet needs is a ceiling: the most you can pay per ₹100 of ad-driven sales before the order loses money.

Think of the budget in three layers:

  • The ceiling comes from your unit economics. That is break-even ROAS, covered below.
  • The starting amount is a test budget you can afford to lose while you learn which campaigns, slots and dishes convert.
  • The scaling rule says when to add money: only while the return stays well above break-even after two to three weeks of data.

Ad spend as a share of online sales then becomes an output, not a target. If 40% of your online sales come through ads at a 5x return, ads cost 8% of total online sales (40 ÷ 5). In PFC's published cases, Drama in Delhi spent about 5.7% of combined net sale on ads before onboarding and about 12.1% after, while combined monthly net sale rose from ₹33.81 lakh to ₹51.32 lakh. Balaji Dhaba's Swiggy ads cost about 2.8% of Swiggy revenue over roughly 10 weeks. Both shares are worked out from the figures on our client results page.

How do you calculate ROAS and break-even ROAS?

ROAS (return on ad spend) is ad-attributed sales divided by ad spend. If ₹10,000 of ads brings ₹50,000 of attributed sales, ROAS is 5x. Swiggy and Zomato dashboards often label this ROI, but it is the same sales-over-spend ratio, not profit.

Break-even ROAS tells you the lowest ROAS at which ads stop losing money. Take ₹100 of sales. Subtract the customer discount you fund, commission and platform fees (including GST), and food plus packaging cost. What is left is your contribution before ads. Break-even ROAS is 100 divided by that contribution.

What ROAS should you expect on Swiggy and Zomato?

From PFC's 2026 growth benchmarks, which use full months only and exclude flagged outliers and part months:

  • Swiggy: platform-reported returns of 2.9x to 16.6x across outlets.
  • Zomato: 1.7x to 9.3x.
  • Head to head: Swiggy's reported return was higher in 22 of 29 matching months across four outlets.

Two cautions apply. First, these are dashboard figures and are not reconciled to net sales. Use them to compare campaigns, not as profit. Second, ad-attributed sales include some orders that would have come anyway. A better test is incremental: how much did sales rise for each extra rupee of ads? Ads budget checklist before you spend the next rupee

  1. Pull your last full month's payout statement and note actual commission, fees and GST per order.
  2. Write down food plus packaging cost as a share of selling price for your top ten dishes.
  3. Calculate contribution per ₹100 and your break-even ROAS for each platform separately.
  4. Fix availability, prep time and menu photos before raising spend. Ads send traffic; the listing has to convert it.
  5. Start a small test on two or three hero dishes or campaigns, with a clear end date.
  6. Read every campaign's terms: billing basis, budget cap, start and end dates, and auto-renewal.
  7. After two to three weeks, compare each campaign's return with your break-even, not with other restaurants.
  8. Scale in steps only where the return holds. Expect returns to drift down as budget grows.
  9. Check incremental sales per added rupee every month, not just the dashboard figure.
  10. Match ad spend changes to payout statements so every deduction is one you agreed to.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 3x ROAS good on Swiggy or Zomato?

It depends on your margins. If you fund a 10% discount, pay about a quarter of sales in commission and fees, and spend 35% on food and packaging, break-even is about 3.3x. At 3x you lose money on ad-driven orders. A lean menu with no discount might break even nearer 2.2x. Work out your own number before judging any campaign.

How are Zomato ads charged?

Zomato's published Sponsored Listing terms bill per click. A click counts when a customer opens your restaurant page from the ad, or within 24 hours of last viewing it. Spend is capped at the campaign budget you agree for set dates and is deducted from weekly payouts. Zomato does not guarantee clicks or results, so track orders and returns yourself.

Should a new cloud kitchen run ads from day one?

A small test can help a new listing get first orders and ratings, but only once the basics are in place: correct menu, good photos, stable availability and reasonable prep time. Ads on a weak listing buy clicks that do not convert. Keep the first budget small and fixed, and measure new customers and conversion before you add money.

Why does my ROAS drop when I increase the budget?

The first rupees usually reach customers already close to ordering. As budget grows, ads reach colder audiences, wider areas and weaker slots, so returns fall. PFC's benchmarks show this drift: one outlet's Zomato return moved from 9.3x on a tiny budget to 4.8x on ₹36.7K. Scale in steps and stop where the return nears break-even.

Is dashboard ROAS the same as profit?

No. Dashboard ROAS divides ad-attributed sales by ad spend. It ignores commission, discounts, food cost and orders that would have come without ads. Use it to compare campaigns against each other. For profit, compare the return with your break-even ROAS and check incremental sales per added rupee against your payout statement.
