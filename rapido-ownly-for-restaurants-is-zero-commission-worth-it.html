Ownly is Rapido's food delivery app. Restaurants pay no commission, listing fee, subscription or marketing charge. Customers pay the menu price plus a delivery fee, and Rapido delivers through its existing rider network.

For a restaurant in a city where it operates, listing is worth it as an extra channel:

  • The per-order economics are strong. Even priced 15% below your aggregator menu, an Ownly order can earn more than 40% more than the same order on Swiggy or Zomato (illustrative maths below).
  • The risks are volume and stability. Ownly is live in only two cities, has shared only headline numbers (about 50,000 daily orders in Bengaluru), and moved from a per-order restaurant fee to zero restaurant fees within its first year.

List on Ownly, price it deliberately, and keep Swiggy and Zomato as your core channels until the volume is proven.

What Ownly Is and Where It Operates

Ownly is run by Rapido and uses Rapido's riders, the same network that runs its bike-taxi and auto business, for delivery. The idea is that riders already on the road, especially in off-peak hours, can deliver food at a lower cost than a dedicated delivery fleet.

What Restaurants Pay

Under the current model, restaurants pay nothing to Ownly per order: no commission, no listing or subscription fee, no marketing charge, and no fee for visibility. Customers pay the delivery fee, which is Ownly's main revenue model. Reports put this fee at a flat ₹30 per order, though earlier coverage described a per-kilometre fee, so check the current structure in the app. At least one report has also mentioned a possible subscription for restaurants, so confirm the terms when you sign up.

The terms have changed within the first year, and that history matters when you plan around them:

Rapido says the model is built to be self-sustaining on delivery fees rather than loss-making. Even so, with zero revenue from restaurants, the terms may not stay this generous. Expect them to change again.

The Risks to Weigh

  • Volume per restaurant is unknown. Rapido has shared city-level figures, not what a typical restaurant gets. Treat early orders as a bonus, not a forecast.
  • Terms can change. A platform that earns nothing from restaurants today may add fees once it has scale. Do not reprice your whole business around today's terms.
  • Food delivery is hard to sustain. Ola's Foodpanda and Uber Eats India both exited despite having large rider networks. Ownly's lower cost base is a real advantage, but not a guarantee.
  • Ownership ties. Swiggy held about 12% of Rapido until it sold its entire stake to Prosus and WestBridge in September 2025, so the two now compete head-on.
  • Customer data. Rapido has talked about giving restaurants tools and data to run their own offers. Confirm exactly what customer data you will receive before treating Ownly as a retention channel.

How to List and Price on Ownly

  1. Check your POS first. Some reports mention Ownly integrations with POS systems including UrbanPiper and Rista; confirm with your POS provider. Taking orders through your existing POS avoids another tablet and missed orders.
  2. Read your aggregator agreements. Check for price-parity or exclusivity clauses before listing lower prices elsewhere. These clauses have drawn regulatory attention: a Competition Commission of India investigation reportedly found that both platforms pushed price parity. If in doubt, ask your lawyer or account manager.
  3. Price deliberately. In our experience, starting 10–15% below your aggregator menu is enough for customers to notice and still earns more per order, as the table above shows.
  4. Use your delivery menu. List the same travel-friendly dishes you sell on the aggregators, not your full dine-in menu.
  5. Protect peak hours. If Ownly orders start delaying your Swiggy and Zomato orders, pause Ownly first. Aggregator rankings punish late and rejected orders. See how the Swiggy ranking algorithm works.
  6. Review after 60 days. Track Ownly orders per week, contribution per order and any effect on aggregator performance, then decide whether to grow it.

Ownly also changes the comparison with your own channel. If you were planning WhatsApp or website ordering mainly to escape commission, compare it with Ownly's economics first. See direct ordering vs Swiggy and Zomato.

Frequently asked questions

Does Rapido Ownly charge restaurants commission?

No. Under the terms in place since March 2026, restaurants pay no commission, listing, subscription or marketing fees. Customers pay the delivery fee. In its first months Ownly charged restaurants a flat per-order fee instead, so check the current terms when you sign up.

Which cities is Ownly available in?

As of 12 November 2026, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Rapido said in September 2026 that it plans to reach all metros in the October–December 2026 quarter, but it has not announced dates for each city.

Is Swiggy an investor in Rapido?

Not any more. Swiggy held about 12% of Rapido and sold its entire stake to Prosus and WestBridge in September 2025, so Ownly competes directly with Swiggy.

How does Ownly make money if restaurants pay nothing?

From the delivery fee customers pay. Rapido says the model is designed to sustain itself on those fees. With no restaurant revenue, though, fees for restaurants could return later, so do not build your whole pricing around today's terms.

Should I price my menu lower on Ownly than on Swiggy and Zomato?

Usually yes. Your aggregator prices include room for commission, which Ownly does not charge. Pricing 10–15% lower still earns more per order and gives customers a reason to choose Ownly. Check your aggregator agreements for price-parity terms first.
