How does the Zomato rating work for delivery and dining?

Zomato split delivery and dining into separate ratings in May 2020, as Business Standard reported at the time. The dining side has since moved. In its May 2025 shareholder letter, Eternal (Zomato's parent) said it expected to finish moving its going-out business to the District app within a couple of quarters, after which going-out would be available only on District. Its October 2026 shareholder letter describes District as the going-out business, covering restaurant discovery, reservations and event ticketing.

So on the Zomato app, your delivery rating is the scorecard that drives orders. If you also run a dine-in outlet, check where your dining reviews show today rather than assume. A strong dining room will not rescue weak delivery packaging.

Zomato does not publish the exact formula. Its content guidelines say the overall rating uses a proprietary weighted average that "factors in recent experiences among other considerations".

What actually moves a Zomato delivery rating?

Order accuracy

A missing raita, the wrong biryani or a forgotten add-on is the fastest route to a one-star rating. Use a printed KOT, a packing checklist at the pass, and a second bag check at peak hours.

Packaging

Gravy leaks, soggy fries and cold food are packaging problems first. Use sealed containers for gravies, vented boxes for fried items and separate compartments for wet and dry food.

Kitchen prep time and delivery time

Customers blame the restaurant for late food, but you control only kitchen prep time (KPT). Keep it honest in the partner app, prep mise en place before peak hours, and mark items out of stock instead of delaying orders.

Portion and value

If the photo shows a full bowl and the customer gets half, the rating drops. Match photos and descriptions to what actually goes in the box. See our Zomato listing optimization guide.

Complaint handling

A complaint handled quickly often stops a bad review. Respond inside the partner app, accept genuine mistakes and fix the cause the same day.

Replying to reviews

A calm reply does not change the star, but it changes how the next customer reads it. Zomato's guidelines ask restaurants not to hit back in replies, and not to offer the customer an incentive to edit the review. See our playbook on handling negative reviews on Zomato without losing rankings.

What does Zomato say about fake and incentivised reviews?

Zomato's rules cover restaurants as well as reviewers. The content guidelines' section for restaurants says: don't solicit reviews. It says the best way to get reviews is to delight customers, and that selective solicitation is a strict no. It also tells restaurants not to:

  • offer freebies, discounts or payment in exchange for reviews;
  • offer incentives for users to remove reviews;
  • ask staff to compete with each other to collect reviews;
  • work with vendors or PR agencies that offer to fix your reviews or ratings.

What happens to restaurants that break these rules? The guidelines say that if Zomato finds direct or indirect evidence of solicitation, it will activate a disclaimer on your restaurant's page telling the Zomato community that your reviews are suspicious. When Zomato launched its new ratings system in 2020, Business Standard reported that restaurants found to have solicited reviews would see a negative impact on their ratings with immediate effect, and that a "suspicious reviews" banner would be added to restaurants found manipulating ratings.

For reviewers, fake or misleading reviews are removed, accepting a kickback could cost them their profile, and joining WhatsApp or Facebook groups that offer incentives for reviews counts as solicitation. The Zomato Terms of Service also bar reviews submitted or removed in exchange for payment, or at the request of the business being reviewed.

There is a protective side too. Reviews written to blackmail a restaurant will be deleted, and so will reviews that show the author never visited or ordered. The guidelines ask restaurants not to give in to customers who demand benefits under threat of a bad review, and not to invite low raters back for a complimentary meal, which they say invites false negative reviews. Report such users to help@zomato.com.

What do India's review rules say?

On 21 November 2022, the Department of Consumer Affairs launched IS 19000:2022, a Bureau of Indian Standards framework titled "Online Consumer Reviews — Principles and Requirements for their Collection, Moderation and Publication". The PIB release on IS 19000:2022 says it applies to every online platform that publishes consumer reviews.

It covers verifying review authors, automated and manual moderation, and how ratings are weighted and displayed. At launch it was voluntary. The release says that once mandatory, violations may count as an unfair trade practice, and consumers can complain to the National Consumer Helpline, Consumer Commissions or the CCPA. Checklist: daily habits that protect your rating

  1. Check the partner app for new complaints and reviews before lunch and before dinner.
  2. Reply to negative reviews the same day, without hitting back, and note the cause.
  3. Run the packing checklist on every order, with a second check at peak hours.
  4. Keep kitchen prep time honest in the partner app.
  5. Mark items out of stock instead of sending substitutes without asking.
  6. Keep the outlet online for the hours you publish.
  7. Place one test order a week, and review complaints with the kitchen every Monday.
  8. Never offer money, food or discounts for reviews, never ask only happy customers, and never run staff review contests.
  9. Say no to any vendor or agency that offers to fix your ratings.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to increase a Zomato rating?

It depends on how many orders you get and how bad the problem is. Zomato says recent experiences carry weight in the rating, so a busy outlet that fixes its complaint causes can see new ratings improve within weeks. A low-volume outlet needs longer because fewer new ratings come in. Track the weekly average of new ratings to see whether your fixes are working.

Can I ask customers to rate my restaurant on Zomato?

Be careful. Zomato's restaurant guidelines say don't solicit reviews, and call selective solicitation, such as asking only happy customers, a strict no. Freebies, discounts or payment for reviews, staff contests and "fix your ratings" vendors are all ruled out. The safe route is a plain thank-you note in every bag with a number to call about problems, no rating request and no reward.

Can I get a fake or unfair review removed?

You can report it. Zomato's content guidelines say reviews written to blackmail a restaurant, reviews that show the author never ordered or visited, and abusive or spam reviews can be removed. The guidelines name help@zomato.com for reporting content and users who demand benefits under threat of a bad review. Zomato does not remove a review just because it is negative, so reply calmly instead.

Does my dining rating affect my delivery rating?

No. Zomato has kept delivery and dining ratings separate since 2020, so a great dining experience will not lift a weak delivery rating. Eternal has also been moving dining-out to its District app, so check where your dining reviews appear now. Delivery problems are usually packaging, accuracy and prep time. Dining problems are usually service, wait time and ambience.

Is it legal to buy reviews in India?

Buying reviews breaks Zomato's Terms of Service, which bar reviews submitted in exchange for payment or at the request of the business being reviewed. Zomato can also put a suspicious-reviews disclaimer on your page. India's IS 19000:2022 standard targets fake reviews too. It started as voluntary, and the government said violations could count as an unfair trade practice if it is made mandatory.
