Why Zomato Ranking Matters

Visibility determines order volume. Customers rarely scroll beyond the first set of search results, and orders are heavily concentrated: the NRAI says 30% of restaurants get 75–80% of orders.

Restaurants that rank higher receive:

  • More impressions
  • More clicks
  • More orders

Higher engagement further strengthens ranking performance, creating a positive growth cycle.

Ad Spend and Ranking Relationship

Paid promotion can influence visibility. However, ads alone do not guarantee long-term ranking improvement; Zomato's own ad terms say it does not guarantee any minimum impressions, clicks or reach.

Also note how Zomato counts a paid click: under its Visit Pack Plus terms, a customer who opens your restaurant page within 24 hours of seeing your ad can count as a click, even if they never tapped the ad.

Role of Ads in Ranking

Frequently asked questions

Does Zomato publish its ranking algorithm?

No. Zomato does not publish how it ranks restaurants, and restaurant bodies such as NRAI have criticised that opacity. The signals in this guide come from patterns in the listings we audit.

What rating do I need to rank well on Zomato?

There is no published cut-off. In our audits, listings below 4.0 typically get reduced visibility, and recent reviews matter as much as the overall average.

Do Zomato ads improve organic ranking?

They can help indirectly by bringing in orders and engagement, but they do not guarantee it. Zomato's ad terms say it does not guarantee minimum impressions, clicks or reach, and ads rarely fix a weak listing.

How long does it take to improve Zomato ranking?

In our client work, photo updates show within 1–2 weeks, menu changes within 2–4 weeks, and rating improvements within 2–6 weeks. Results vary by city and competition.

Do cancellations hurt my Zomato ranking?

Zomato does not say how cancellations affect ranking, but in our experience unreliable preparation and cancellations reduce visibility. They also cost money directly, because Zomato's partner terms let it recover refunds and costs for cancelled orders from the restaurant.
