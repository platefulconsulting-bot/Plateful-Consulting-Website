Moisture Control and Texture Preservation

Steam build-up inside a closed box reduces food quality. Common examples:

  • soggy fries
  • soft fried chicken
  • wet sandwiches

Packaging should allow limited ventilation where the dish needs it.

Moisture Control Techniques

  • perforated lids
  • vent holes
  • absorbent liners
  • compartment separation

Food that arrives with the right texture is more likely to be reordered.

Packaging SOP (Standard Operating Process)

Consistent packaging needs a structured workflow.

Basic Packaging SOP

  1. Verify item quantity.
  2. Check the lid seal.
  3. Apply the tamper sticker.
  4. Use the correct bag size.
  5. Confirm order accuracy.

Standardisation improves consistency across shifts and staff.

Packaging Optimisation Framework

Step 1: Identify common packaging issues

Read recent reviews and complaints and list the packaging problems that come up most.

Step 2: Test leak-proof containers

Trial containers suited to each food type before switching fully.

Step 3: Standardise the packaging process

Write down the packing SOP so every order is packed the same way.

Step 4: Train staff on the packing workflow

Make sure everyone at the packing station follows the SOP.

Step 5: Review feedback regularly

Check customer feedback every 2–3 months and adjust.

Continuous improvement keeps the customer experience consistent.

Frequently asked questions

How does packaging affect Swiggy and Zomato ratings?

Poor packaging often leads to complaints about leakage, temperature and presentation, which pull ratings down even when the food tastes good.

What is the best packaging for hot food delivery?

Insulated containers with secure lids help hold temperature and prevent leakage. Foil sealing, double-layer containers and thermal dispatch bags also help.

How much should packaging cost per order?

Typical packaging cost is about ₹10–₹20 for cloud kitchens, ₹15–₹30 for casual dining and ₹25–₹40 for premium brands.

Should packaging include branding?

Yes. Branding improves recall and the customer experience, but it should stay cost-effective. Stickers, printed tape and inserts are low-cost ways to start.

How often should packaging materials be reviewed?

Review packaging quality every 2–3 months based on customer feedback.
