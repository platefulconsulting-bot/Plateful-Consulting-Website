Frequently asked questions

Is it mandatory to be on both Swiggy and Zomato?

No, but presence on both often increases visibility and order potential. Restaurants with limited kitchen capacity, a highly niche cuisine or strong ranking on one platform can do well on a single app.

Do I need separate menus for Swiggy and Zomato?

Menus can be similar but optimised based on each platform's performance data. Keep item names, menu structure and photos consistent so customers recognise your brand on both apps.

Can I have different pricing on each platform?

Yes, for reasons such as commission differences, promotions or demand, but the differences should stay consistent and logical. Large gaps can reduce trust.

Will managing both platforms increase workload?

Yes, menu updates, pricing checks, inventory sync and order coordination all double up. Integrating your POS with aggregator dashboards and standardising kitchen workflow helps manage the extra complexity.

Which platform should I start with?

The choice depends on your cuisine category and local demand patterns. Optimise the first platform properly before launching the second.
