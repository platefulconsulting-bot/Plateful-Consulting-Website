Frequently asked questions

Can restaurants get fake reviews removed on Zomato?

You can report reviews that look fake, unrelated, duplicate or abusive, and Zomato may reassess them. Removal decisions depend on Zomato's content guidelines and its own fake-review checks; genuine negative experiences are not removed simply because they are negative.

How fast should I respond to a negative review?

Within 24 hours. A prompt, polite reply shows accountability to the reviewer and to every future customer reading the listing.

Do negative reviews permanently affect ranking?

No. Occasional negative reviews do not permanently hurt a listing if the overall rating stays strong. Repeated, unaddressed complaints on the same theme are what tend to weigh on visibility.

Should I respond to every review?

A consistent response habit improves brand perception. At minimum, reply to every negative review using a standard, polite template.

Can improving packaging reduce complaints?

Packaging is a frequent complaint theme, and improvements to leakage, damage and temperature loss often reduce negative feedback on those issues. The size of any improvement varies by restaurant.
