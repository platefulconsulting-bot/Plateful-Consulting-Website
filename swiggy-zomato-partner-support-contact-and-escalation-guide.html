Is there an official Swiggy partner customer care number?

Not one we could verify on a current, publicly readable Swiggy page. Swiggy's partner FAQ now sits behind the partner login, and Swiggy's corporate contact page lists no phone numbers at all. What Swiggy does publish is an email. Its Stakeholder Engagement and Grievance Redressal Policy names partnersupport@swiggy.in as the grievance channel for restaurant partners.

The Swiggy Partner app's own store listing tells owners who cannot log in to ask their Swiggy POC (point of contact) for access. So in practice your three Swiggy channels are the Partner app, partnersupport@swiggy.in and your account manager or POC. If a Swiggy partner contact number is shown to you inside the logged-in Partner app or partner portal, that is the one to trust. Do not use any number you found on a directory website.

How do you contact Zomato restaurant partner customer care?

Zomato does not publish a restaurant-partner phone line on the public pages we could check. In our work with partner outlets, day-to-day Zomato partner support runs through the help or support section of the Zomato Restaurant Partner app, plus your account manager if one is assigned. The app's store listing describes it as the place to manage orders, item stock and category timings, so menu and availability problems can be raised from the same app where you see them.

For a formal written complaint, Zomato's Terms of Service (last updated 19 February 2026) name a Grievance Officer at Eternal Limited, reachable at grievance@zomato.com, and a Nodal Officer at nodal@zomato.com. That section is written mainly for customer order complaints. The phone lines it lists are for customers, not a dedicated Zomato restaurant partner customer care line. Use the grievance email only after the in-app help section and your account manager have failed, and always quote your ticket numbers.

What should you include in a support ticket?

Most tickets bounce back because the agent cannot find the order or cannot see the problem. A complete ticket saves days. Follow this checklist every time:

  1. Outlet or restaurant ID. Copy it from the app. If you run several outlets, name the exact one.
  2. Order IDs. List every affected order, not "many orders last week".
  3. Dates and time window. The exact dates, or the payout cycle, the problem covers.
  4. What you expected and what happened. For money issues, show the figure you expected, the figure you received and the gap.
  5. Screenshots. Payout statement lines, the menu edit you submitted, the outlet status screen, the review in question.
  6. Supporting documents. FSSAI licence, GST certificate or bank proof when the issue touches compliance or bank details.
  7. One issue per ticket. Mixed tickets get partly answered and closed.
  8. Ticket number log. Note every ticket number, date raised and reply in one sheet. You will need it to escalate.

How to escalate an unresolved issue

Escalate in steps. Jumping straight to a legal notice usually slows things down.

  1. Reopen the ticket with a clear reply: what is still wrong and what you want done.
  2. Message your account manager with the ticket number, outlet ID and a two-line summary. Account managers can often push a stuck ticket internally. Our piece on consultants vs agencies vs platform account managers explains what they can and cannot do.
  3. Write to the published email. For Swiggy, partnersupport@swiggy.in. For Zomato, the grievance address in its Terms. Put the outlet ID and ticket numbers in the subject line.
  4. Read your partner agreement. It sets out how disputes are handled. Read that clause before you take any legal step.
  5. Use your industry body for wider issues. When a policy hits many restaurants, associations step in. In 2025, Inc42 reported the National Restaurant Association of India taking up restaurants' complaints about Zomato's long-distance fee directly with Eternal's leadership.

Swiggy's grievance policy also lists an integrity portal for ethical misconduct and anti-bribery matters. If anyone claiming to be from a platform asks for a personal payment to "fix" your listing or rates, do not pay. Report it through official channels.

Beware of fake Swiggy and Zomato customer care numbers

Fake helpline numbers are a known scam. They appear in search results, map listings and directory sites. In August 2025, Digit reported that a search for a Swiggy customer care number in Google's AI Mode showed numbers that were not meant for customers or were not on Swiggy's official site. The same report notes that Swiggy says it has no official customer care phone lines. Police advisories have warned against searching for helpline numbers on Google for years.

Restaurant owners are targets too, because "partner support" calls about payouts or deactivation create urgency. Simple rules:

  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, net banking passwords or card details with anyone claiming to be support.
  • Never install a screen-sharing or "remote support" app on a caller's request.
  • Never pay a "reactivation" or "verification" fee to a personal account or UPI ID.
  • Confirm any unexpected call through your account manager or an in-app ticket before acting.
  • Change bank details only inside the partner app, never over a call or chat link.

When support is not the real problem

Many owners raise tickets for a sudden fall in orders. Often nothing is broken. Ranking, ratings, availability or a competitor's offers have shifted. Before escalating, go through the checks in our guide on why Swiggy and Zomato orders drop. Support teams can fix a technical fault. They rarely fix a growth problem.

How do I raise a ticket with Zomato partner support?

What should I do if a Swiggy or Zomato payout looks wrong?

First, rebuild the payout yourself from the statement: order value, commission, taxes, ad spend, discounts and any refund or penalty lines. Then raise an in-app ticket naming the exact lines you dispute, with order IDs and screenshots. If nothing moves, send the ticket number to your account manager. On Swiggy, you can also write to partnersupport@swiggy.in with the same evidence.

How can I tell if a support call is fake?

Treat any call asking for an OTP, UPI PIN, card details, a screen-sharing app or a payment to a personal account as a scam. Platforms do not need these to fix a listing or payout. Hang up and check through an in-app ticket or your account manager. Never trust a number just because it appeared at the top of a search result.
