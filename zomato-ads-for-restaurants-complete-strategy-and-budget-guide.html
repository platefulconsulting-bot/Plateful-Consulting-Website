How Zomato Ads Work

Zomato sells sponsored listings on a cost-per-click basis, typically ₹5–15 per click according to Inc42, alongside banner-style formats such as Brand Tiles and Branding on Search. Inc42 puts banner pricing at roughly ₹30–300 per thousand impressions.

Note how a click is counted: under Zomato's CPC terms, a customer who opens your restaurant page within 24 hours of seeing your ad can count as a paid click, even if they never tapped the ad. Budget with that in mind, because some of those visits might have happened anyway.

Zomato decides the size, placement and positioning of ads at its own discretion. In our experience, placement also tracks:

  • Bid competitiveness
  • Relevance to the search query
  • Listing performance signals

Higher engagement improves campaign efficiency over time. Ad invoices are usually settled by deduction from your weekly payouts, so check them on your payout statement.

ROAS Optimisation Framework

Return on ad spend (ROAS) measures campaign efficiency.

ROAS formula

ROAS = revenue generated ÷ ad spend

Example: ₹4,000 revenue from ₹1,000 spend = 4x ROAS.

ROAS is based on revenue, not profit. At 25% or higher commission, a 3x ROAS can still lose money once commission, GST, food and packaging are paid. We show the worked maths in our Swiggy Ads strategy guide; the same logic applies on Zomato.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Zomato ads cost?

Sponsored listings are billed per click, reported at about ₹5–15 per click, and banner formats at roughly ₹30–300 per thousand impressions. Most restaurants we work with start with daily budgets between ₹200 and ₹600, depending on competition and category demand.

What counts as a click on Zomato ads?

Under Zomato's CPC terms, a click includes a customer reaching your restaurant page within 24 hours of viewing your ad, not only a tap on the ad itself. Factor that into how you read your cost per click.

What ROAS should I expect?

In our client accounts, strong campaigns typically reach 3x–5x ROAS, but Zomato does not guarantee results and neither can anyone else. Check your break-even ROAS first, because at 25%+ commission a 3x ROAS can still lose money.

Are Zomato sponsored listings worth it?

They often give a good return when the menu is optimised and ratings are strong. On a weak listing, paid clicks convert poorly, so fix the fundamentals first.

How long before Zomato ads show results?

In our experience, initial performance insights usually appear within 7–14 days.

Should new restaurants run ads?

Ads can speed up visibility in the early growth phase, as long as you start with a small test budget and track cost per order.
