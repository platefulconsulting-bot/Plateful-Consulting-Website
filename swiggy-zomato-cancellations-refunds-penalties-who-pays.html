With Zomato cancelled orders and Swiggy refunds, whoever caused the problem pays. If your kitchen rejects an order, lets it time out or sends a wrong or missing item, expect to lose the order value and sometimes pay a penalty. If the customer or rider caused it, you should not be charged, and you can dispute the deduction.

This guide goes through each scenario using the platforms' own published terms, where they exist. Where a platform has not published a rule, we say so. Your partner agreement is the final word.

Who pays when an order is cancelled, rejected or refunded?

Both platforms decide cost by fault. Zomato's restaurant-partner terms are public on its parent company's site as the Eternal online ordering terms (last updated February 2023), and they go into detail. Swiggy's restaurant contract is not public, but its customer-facing cancellation and refund policy shows how it treats each case from the customer's side.

What happens when the restaurant rejects or cancels?

You lose the order value and the customer gets a refund. On Zomato there is a second cost once you cross the weekly threshold: the rejected-order charge. A time-out counts the same as a rejection. A tablet that goes quiet during the dinner rush costs you exactly what a deliberate reject does.

Zomato's terms also let it cancel an order when the restaurant cannot communicate its response in time. They ask you to switch off "Accepting Delivery" when you cannot serve. Going offline for an hour is cheaper than rejecting ten orders.

On Swiggy, restaurant-side charges for rejections sit in your partner agreement, not on a public page. Check the agreement and your weekly invoice for any line linked to rejected or cancelled orders.

Out-of-stock items: why calling the customer is not a fix

When an item runs out mid-service, many kitchens accept the order and then call the customer to swap or cancel. That turns a stock problem into a cancellation, a refund and often a low rating. Under Swiggy's policy, the customer can cancel the whole order if one item is unavailable. On Zomato, an accepted order you cannot fulfil counts as a rejected order.

Mark items out of stock the moment they run short and switch them back on when restocked. If you use a POS integration, check that stock toggles reach both apps.

Customer complaints: missing item, wrong item, quality, spillage

Complaint refunds are where most money leaks. The customer raises a complaint, the platform refunds part or all of the order, and the amount is recovered from you if the fault is judged to be yours. Zomato's terms add ₹10 per order on all "orders requiring support" in any week where they exceed 5% of weekly orders. These include delays, poor quality, missing or wrong items and poor packaging.

Spillage is usually treated as a packing problem. Zomato requires food to be packed and fastened to avoid spillage. Swiggy's policy gives customers a proportionate refund if packaging is tampered or damaged and they refuse it at the door. Tamper-evident seals protect you on both apps. Late delivery and customer cancellations

If you handed the food over on time and the rider was late, the delay is not yours. Zomato puts delay costs on the restaurant where late handover caused them, so your handover time is your evidence. Mark food ready honestly and on time.

Customer cancellations after acceptance usually cost the customer, not you. Swiggy's policy lets it charge the customer up to the full order amount. When Zomato relaunched its opt-in Food Rescue feature in November 2024, which offers cancelled orders to nearby customers, it said restaurants would still be compensated for the original order. If a customer cancellation is recovered from your payout, raise it.

How to dispute a wrong deduction

  1. Find the order ID. Match the deduction line in the weekly statement to the order. Do it weekly; old orders are harder to prove.
  2. Collect evidence. A time-stamped packing photo or CCTV clip, the KOT, a photo of the seal, and the food-ready and rider pickup times from the partner app.
  3. Raise a ticket in the partner app. Give the order ID, the amount, what you dispute and why. Attach the evidence.
  4. Note the ticket number and follow up. If it closes without an answer, reopen it with the same evidence.
  5. Escalate. Go to your account manager if you have one, or to partner support. Batch repeat issues into one monthly log rather than scattered complaints.
  6. Check the reversal. Confirm the credit appears in a later payout. If it does not, quote the ticket number in your next escalation.

For problems that hit many restaurants, industry bodies such as NRAI have taken them up directly with the platforms, as the 2026 Bengaluru talks showed.

Prevention checklist

  1. Stock sync. Mark items out of stock the moment they run short, on both apps, and check that POS sync works.
  2. Honest prep times. Set preparation time to what the kitchen manages at peak, not on a quiet afternoon.
  3. Auto-accept with care. Use it only if the kitchen can genuinely cook every order that comes in. Otherwise it turns rejections into late or unfulfilled orders.
  4. Go offline, don't reject. Switch off ordering when short-staffed, out of key stock or closing early.
  5. Tamper-proof packaging. Seal stickers, spill-proof containers and separate packing for gravies and liquids.
  6. Packing photos or CCTV. Photograph each sealed order with its order slip visible, or keep a camera over the packing station.
  7. Item count at handover. Check the bag against the KOT before the rider leaves.
  8. Weekly review. Track rejections, complaints and deductions by reason, and fix the top cause first.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays when Zomato cancels an order because the restaurant did not respond?

The restaurant. Zomato's published terms count an order that times out without your response as a rejected order, the same as a manual reject. You are not paid for it. If your rejected orders cross 0.5% of weekly volume, Zomato's terms add a charge of 10% of their net order value, rising to 25% above 2%. Check your current agreement for the exact figures that apply to you.

Does the restaurant get paid if a customer cancels a Swiggy order?

Swiggy's customer policy allows it to charge the customer up to 100% of the order amount for a cancellation after ordering, and says this is to compensate merchants and delivery partners. How much reaches you depends on your partner agreement and when the cancellation happened. Check the order in your settlement. If a customer cancellation shows a full recovery from you, raise a ticket with the order ID.

Can Swiggy or Zomato recover a complaint refund from my payout?

Yes, when the fault is judged to be yours. Zomato's terms say you are not paid for orders refunded because of poor quality or not following delivery instructions, and the amount can be set off against later payouts. Swiggy handles this through your partner agreement. In both cases you can dispute a recovery you think is wrong, using the order ID and packing evidence.

What evidence works best when disputing a missing-item complaint?

A time-stamped photo or CCTV clip of the sealed bag with the order slip visible is the strongest. Add the KOT showing every item, a photo of the tamper seal, and the food-ready and pickup times from the partner app. Zomato's terms limit its delivery responsibility to tallying the item count, not the contents of an untampered pack, so proof of a sealed, complete bag matters.

Do cancellations and rejections affect my ranking and rating?

Neither platform publishes its ranking formula, but reliability clearly matters. Rejections, time-outs and complaint-heavy weeks mean fewer completed orders, more unhappy customers and lower ratings, all of which work against your visibility. Zomato's terms also add charges once rejections or support-heavy orders cross set thresholds. Keeping cancellations close to zero is one of the cheapest ways to protect both ranking and margin.
