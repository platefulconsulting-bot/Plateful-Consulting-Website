Delivery + Dine-In Hybrid Strategy

Many Bangalore restaurants operate both dine-in and delivery channels. Balancing both revenue streams improves stability.

Hybrid Growth Approach

  • optimise the delivery menu separately from dine-in
  • align pricing strategy across channels
  • improve listing visibility on discovery platforms
  • optimise dine-in positioning on Zomato

Delivery pricing has to account for the platform commission in your own Swiggy and Zomato contracts. Pricing, menu and offer decisions should be built around these legitimate costs rather than ignoring them.

An integrated strategy across both channels improves total revenue.
