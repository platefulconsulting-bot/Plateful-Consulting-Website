Who can register a restaurant on Zomato?

Any business that can legally sell food can apply: restaurants, QSRs, cafes, bakeries, sweet shops, cloud kitchens and home kitchens. Format matters less than paperwork. Zomato verifies the legal entity, the food safety registration for that address, and a bank account in the right name.

What documents do you need for Zomato registration?

The Zomato partner sign-up form asks for documents in sections: PAN, GST, FSSAI, bank details, menu and photos. Uploads are accepted as JPG, PNG or PDF, each below 5 MB. The form also spells out its matching rules, which are where most applications get stuck.

Can you do Zomato registration without GST or an FSSAI licence?

Without GST: in many cases, yes. The form treats the GST certificate as "if applicable", and it has a declaration for partners who are "not required to obtain registration under the GST law" because they are below the turnover threshold. Zomato's online ordering terms also say that from 1 January 2022, under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act, Zomato pays the tax on restaurant services ordered through it. But note two warnings from the form. Your application will be rejected if a GST registration is already mapped to the PAN you enter. And if your GST is registered in another state, you cannot proceed until you have a GSTIN in the outlet's state. Ask your CA before you choose the non-GST route.

Without FSSAI: no, not for long. Zomato's terms require partners to furnish an FSSAI licence and keep all licences valid, and they allow delisting if you don't. The form links to the FSSAI portal and mentions the "certificate or application", so a pending application may get you started, but you need the registration to trade. FSSAI raised its turnover bands in 2026: basic registration now covers businesses up to ₹1.5 crore a year and a state licence covers turnover above that up to ₹50 crore. Check your category on the FoSCoS portal before you apply.

Zomato's terms define a one-time, non-refundable sign-up fee "set out in the Form", and the current sign-up flow includes a plan and payment step. We could not find a published 2026 amount, so we won't quote one. It varies by city and plan and is shown during sign-up before you pay.

How to register your restaurant on Zomato, step by step

  1. Collect your documents and check that names and addresses match across all of them.
  2. Open the partner sign-up. Go to the Zomato "Partner with us" page on the web, or download the Zomato Restaurant Partner app.
  3. Add outlet details. Restaurant name, exact address, map pin, contact numbers, cuisines and opening hours. Place the map pin on the kitchen itself.
  4. Fill the PAN and GST sections. Enter the legal entity name exactly as on the PAN. If GST exists on that PAN, enter it.
  5. Fill the FSSAI section and upload the certificate.
  6. Add bank details. The beneficiary name must match your legal or trade name.
  7. Upload your menu and photos. Use your own food photos.
  8. Choose your plan and delivery model. Review the service fee shown for Zomato delivery versus your own delivery, plus any sign-up fee.
  9. Review and sign the partner contract. Read the fee section carefully before accepting it.
  10. Wait for verification. Answer calls quickly and fix anything sent back.
  11. Go live and set up the app. Log in to the Zomato Restaurant Partner app, test order acceptance on the tablet or phone, and switch the outlet online.

Why Zomato applications get rejected or delayed

  • FSSAI address mismatch. The form requires the FSSAI address to match the restaurant address exactly.
  • Name mismatch. The FSSAI name does not match the restaurant name, the PAN entity or the GST trade name.
  • Undisclosed GST. A GST registration exists on your PAN but you applied as non-GST.
  • GST in the wrong state. Your GSTIN is registered in a different state from the outlet.
  • Bank name mismatch. The account holder name differs from your legal or trade name.
  • Blurred or cropped uploads. Unreadable documents get sent back for re-upload.
  • Stock photos. Menu images detected as stock get flagged.
  • Duplicate listing. An old listing for the same outlet already exists. Claim it instead of creating a new one.

Your first 30 days after Zomato registration

New listings are judged quickly on ratings, prep time and acceptance. Our checklist:

  1. Week 1: fix the listing. Real photos, clear item names and short descriptions. Our Zomato listing optimisation guide covers each field.
  2. Week 1: set realistic prep times. Use measured kitchen times.

  5. Week 2 to 3: build early ratings. Reply calmly to every negative review.
  6. Week 3: test one offer. Keep it simple and on best-margin items.
  7. Week 4: read your first payouts. Settlement is weekly by default under the terms. Check commission, ads, discounts and GST on every statement.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zomato registration free in 2026?

Not necessarily. Zomato's partner terms define a one-time, non-refundable sign-up fee that is set out in the sign-up form. The amount varies by city and plan, and you see it during sign-up before paying. After going live, the main cost is the per-order service fee, which depends on your contract.

Can I register on Zomato without GST?

Often, yes. The sign-up form treats GST as "if applicable" and has a declaration for partners below the GST turnover threshold. Since 1 January 2022, Zomato pays tax on restaurant services ordered through it under Section 9(5). However, Zomato rejects the application if a GST registration is already linked to your PAN and you leave it out. Your GSTIN must also be in the outlet's state. Confirm with your CA.

Can I sell on Zomato without an FSSAI licence?

No. Zomato's terms require an FSSAI licence or registration and allow delisting if you do not keep it valid. The sign-up form links to the FSSAI portal if you have not applied, but you need the registration in hand to trade. From 2026, basic FSSAI registration covers food businesses with turnover up to ₹1.5 crore a year.

It depends on your contract, city and whether Zomato or your own staff deliver. The form shows separate service fee rates for each delivery model.
