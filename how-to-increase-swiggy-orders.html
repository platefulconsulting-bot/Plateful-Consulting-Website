If you want to know how to increase Swiggy orders, work the funnel in order. First get seen, then get your menu opened, then get the cart checked out, then get the customer back. In practice that means fixing availability and the listing first, then menu and pricing, then ratings. Scale ads only after that.

Quick answer

  • Stay online for every hour you list and keep cancellations close to zero. Missed orders cost you the sale and your standing.
  • Fix the listing: correct cuisine tags, real photos of best sellers, clear dish descriptions.
  • Cut the menu to dishes that sell and travel well. Add combos to lift order value.
  • Repair ratings in the kitchen and in packaging, not only in review replies.
  • Run Swiggy ads after the basics are fixed. Judge them on profit per order, not ROAS alone.
  • Check the ads and discount lines on your payout statement every week.

Why are your Swiggy orders stuck?

Swiggy is a crowded shelf. In its Q4 FY2026 shareholder letter, Swiggy reported about 2.75 lakh average monthly transacting restaurant partners in food delivery and 18.3 million average monthly transacting food delivery users in that quarter. All of them compete for the same scroll.

The same letter says Swiggy has grown its take-rate by deepening its advertising ecosystem. Paid visibility is now a normal part of the platform. You do not have to spend first, but organic gains must come from what the platform can measure: time online, conversion, delivery and ratings.

Swiggy does not publish its ranking formula. What we describe below comes from audits of client dashboards, not from an official document. Ads sit on top of four basics: availability, listing, menu and ratings.

Lever 1: Availability and operations come first

An outlet that is offline cannot be found. Keep your listed hours equal to your real hours. If the kitchen closes early, change the hours instead of switching off mid-shift.

In PFC client data, Balaji Dhaba ran at 99% availability on Swiggy, and Olantic 8 reached 99%+ on both apps from its fourth month. Mohan Sweets went from 77.9% availability on Zomato in the month before PFC to 99%+ by its second month. Track three numbers weekly: time online, order acceptance and cancellations. Prep time matters too. At Olantic 8, kitchen prep time on Zomato came down from 26 minutes to 11 minutes.

Lever 2: Fix the Swiggy listing

The listing decides whether a customer taps your outlet or the one below it.

  • Cuisine tags: use every tag that honestly matches what you sell and what customers search for.
  • Photos: shoot your top 10 dishes first, in natural light, plated as they arrive in the box.
  • Descriptions: name the main ingredient, the taste and the portion size. "Serves 1" beats "delicious".
  • Menu hygiene: remove items you often run out of. Mark best sellers.
  • Minimum order value: set it at what one person realistically orders.

Our Swiggy listing optimization guide covers each field in detail.

Lever 3: Build a delivery menu, not a copy of dine-in

Long dine-in menus rarely work on an app screen. A focused delivery menu of dishes that travel well is easier to scan and easier for the kitchen to deliver consistently. Put best sellers that also carry good margin at the top. Follow with combos, then core items, then add-ons and drinks.

Combos are the simplest way to lift order value. A main, a side and a drink, priced a little below the items bought separately, gives the customer a reason to spend more. Watch your average order value after launch. At Olantic 8, order value held at around ₹330–355 after the first month, so its growth came from more orders, not bigger ones.

On pricing, most restaurants price delivery a little above dine-in to cover packaging, commission and fees. Commission is set in your contract, so the right mark-up is specific to you. Press reports in late 2025 put Swiggy commissions at roughly 17% to 25% depending on the contract, and Swiggy also announced a per-order fee for select restaurants linked to their share of Swiggy One orders. Read your own agreement and payout statement before setting prices.

Lever 4: Use offers that pay for themselves

Discounts can buy orders, but they can also buy losses. Ask how much profit is left per order after discount, commission and food cost.

Punjabi Rasoi in Rohini shows the alternative. Its effective Swiggy discount fell from 27.3% to 13.4% while Swiggy sales per month rose from ₹7.85 lakh to ₹13.53 lakh. Olantic 8 shared discounts with Swiggy at 70:30 and added ₹99 items and minimum-order freebies. Before you run any offer, model it in our discount calculator.

Lever 5: Ratings are an operations problem

Ratings follow the food. A weak rating gives customers a reason to scroll past, and it rarely recovers through replies alone. Fix the cause first.

  • Late food: set realistic prep times and keep to them.
  • Spills and soggy food: upgrade containers by dish type.
  • Wrong or missing items: add a check at handover.
  • Taste varies: write recipes and portion rules for every shift.

Reply to reviews calmly and quickly. Never buy or swap reviews. The Burrito Bros moved its Zomato rating from 1.8 to 4.3 over a six-month plan through rating drives and complaint handling, and its prep time on Zomato fell from 14 minutes to 8. See our guide on taking a Swiggy rating from 3.8 to 4.5.

Lever 6: Swiggy ads, done with numbers

In August 2026, Swiggy launched Guru, an AI assistant for restaurant partners, and said partners can use it to start, review and modify their ad campaigns through a chat prompt. Confirm the billing basis and current rates in your Partner app or with your account manager, because they can change.

Three rules keep ads honest:

  • Fix first, spend second. Ads send more people to your menu. If the menu or rating is weak, you pay to show them a weak menu.
  • Know your break-even ROAS. ROAS is based on revenue, not profit. As an example, if you keep 25% of order value after food cost, packaging, commission and discount, every ₹1 of ads needs about ₹4 of sales just to break even. Work out what you keep per order in our commission calculator.
  • Approve every campaign yourself. In 2026, Bengaluru restaurant associations demanded a freeze on ad spends run without explicit written consent. Inc42 reported, citing Economic Times sources, that Swiggy agreed to refund promotional charges allegedly levied without consent. Check the ads line on every payout.

Lever 7: Know Swiggy's own growth programmes

Swiggy's Q4 FY2026 letter names Bolt (fast delivery), the 99 Store (affordable meals) and Eat Right (health-focused) among its growth offerings. It says its speed and affordability propositions together make up about one fourth of platform volumes. If your dishes fit one of these, ask your account manager about eligibility and terms. Price the dish so it still makes money at that price point, and read any Swiggy One-linked fee in your agreement.

How to increase Swiggy orders: a 30-day checklist

  1. Pull four weeks of dashboard data and mark the weakest funnel stage.
  2. Match listed hours to real kitchen hours. Aim for 99%+ online time.
  3. Remove dishes you often run out of and fix cuisine tags.
  4. Photograph and rewrite descriptions for your top 10 dishes.
  5. Reorder the menu: best sellers, combos, core items, add-ons, drinks.
  6. Launch two or three combos and track order value weekly.
  7. List your top three complaint types and fix the kitchen or packaging cause of each.
  8. Calculate break-even ROAS and profit per order before any ad or offer.
  9. Start a small ad test on best sellers. Review it every few days.
  10. Reconcile ads, discounts and fees on every weekly payout.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to increase Swiggy orders?

Listing changes such as photos, tags and descriptions usually show within days. Menu and pricing changes need two to four weeks of data to judge. Ratings move slowest, because they depend on many new orders going well. Most outlets should judge a plan over at least one to two months, comparing like-for-like weeks so festivals or weather do not distort the picture.

Can I grow Swiggy orders without running ads?

Yes, to a point. Availability, a clean listing, a focused menu and better ratings all lift orders without ad spend, and they make every later rupee of ads work harder. In busy categories, though, competitors are buying visibility. Most outlets end up using a small, controlled ad budget once the basics are fixed and break-even ROAS is known.

What ROAS should I aim for on Swiggy ads?

There is no single right number. ROAS measures revenue, not profit, so the target depends on your food cost, packaging, commission and discount. Work out the share of each order you keep, then divide one by it to get break-even ROAS. Anything above that adds profit. Use platform-reported ROAS as a guide and check it against your payout.

How much commission does Swiggy charge restaurants?

Commission depends on your contract, city and services chosen. Press reports from late 2025 put it at roughly 17% to 25%, and select restaurants may pay an extra per-order fee linked to their share of Swiggy One orders. There is no hidden rate to unlock. Read your agreement and weekly payout statement to see what you actually pay.

Why did my Swiggy orders suddenly drop?

Start with the basics. Check whether the outlet went offline, whether a duplicate listing appeared, whether the rating fell, whether an offer or ad ended, or whether a nearby competitor launched. Compare impressions, menu opens and orders week by week to see which stage broke. The Burrito Bros saw a Swiggy dip from a duplicate-outlet issue that was later resolved.
