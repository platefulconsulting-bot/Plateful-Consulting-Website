A restaurant consultant works out why a restaurant is not growing or not making enough profit, and then fixes it. In practice that means analysing performance data and putting structured changes in place across Swiggy and Zomato listings, menu pricing and AOV, food and packaging costs, and growth strategy, then tracking measurable outcomes instead of handing over generic advice.

This typically includes:

  • Optimising Swiggy and Zomato performance
  • Improving menu pricing and AOV
  • Reducing cost inefficiencies
  • Designing growth strategies

A good consultant does not just give advice. They identify problems, implement solutions and track measurable outcomes.

Services a Restaurant Consultant Provides

The exact services vary, but good consultants typically work across the following areas.

1. Platform Optimisation (Swiggy & Zomato)

This includes:

  • Improving listing visibility
  • Fixing ranking issues
  • Optimising menus for delivery
  • Managing ads and promotions

This is one of the highest-impact areas for modern restaurants.

2. Menu Engineering

Consultants analyse:

  • Item-level profitability
  • Sales contribution
  • Customer preferences

and redesign the menu to:

  • Increase AOV
  • Improve margins
  • Reduce complexity

3. Pricing Strategy

Pricing is adjusted to account for:

  • Platform commission
  • Customer perception
  • Competitive positioning

Pricing work uses legitimate levers such as delivery-specific pricing, item mix and offer structure. Even small pricing changes can have a noticeable effect on profit.

4. Cost & Profitability Analysis

Consultants break down:

  • Food cost
  • Packaging cost
  • Commission
  • Ad spend

to determine actual profit per order.

5. Operations & Process Improvement

This includes:

  • Reducing order errors
  • Improving preparation time
  • Enhancing consistency

Ratings come from customers, so no consultant can change them directly. What a restaurant can do is reduce errors, speed up preparation and keep food consistent, which are the things customers rate and the reasons they reorder.

6. Growth Strategy & Expansion

Advanced consultants assist with:

  • Cloud kitchen setup
  • Multi-brand strategy
  • Market expansion

Frequently asked questions

What does a restaurant consultant do day to day?

They analyse performance data and then implement changes: optimising Swiggy and Zomato listings and ads, re-engineering the menu, adjusting pricing for commission and competition, breaking down cost per order, and improving operations such as order accuracy and preparation time.

How much does a restaurant consultant cost in India?

A one-time audit typically costs ₹10,000 – ₹50,000 and monthly consulting ₹25,000 – ₹1,50,000. Performance-based fees vary. Pricing depends on the consultant's experience, scope of services, restaurant size and engagement length.

What is the difference between a general and an aggregator-focused consultant?

General consultants focus on offline areas such as interior design, staff training and kitchen setup. Aggregator-focused consultants work on Swiggy and Zomato optimisation, delivery menu strategy, ad ROI and platform ranking, which drive online revenue.

What results can I expect from hiring a consultant?

Restaurants working with structured consultants often achieve 30–100% more orders and 20–50% higher AOV, along with better margins. These figures are illustrative; results vary and are not guaranteed.

When is a consultant not worth it?

If you are still in very early experimentation, running a small hobby kitchen, or already have strong in-house expertise, a consultant may not add enough to justify the fee.
