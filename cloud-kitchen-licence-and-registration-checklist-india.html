Cloud kitchen registration in India comes down to three licences almost every owner needs: an FSSAI registration or licence, a shop and establishment registration (or local trade licence), and GST registration once your turnover crosses the threshold. A fire NOC, pollution consent, signage permission and a trade mark depend on your building, size and city.

FSSAI licence for restaurants: which category applies in 2026?

FSSAI revised its turnover thresholds this year. The amendment regulations were notified on 10 March 2026 and the new limits took effect on 1 April 2026, as set out in FSSAI's FAQs of 27 March 2026. Most new cloud kitchens now fall under basic registration, not a state licence.

A few practical points. The registration is tied to the premises, so a second kitchen address needs its own. Some activities need a licence regardless of turnover, so check the category list on FoSCoS. Licences and registrations now have perpetual validity, but you still pay the annual fee and stay compliant. Basic registration costs ₹100 a year. State licence fees depend on your kind of business, so check the FoSCoS fee screen for your exact amount. If the new thresholds move you to a different category, FSSAI says no fee applies for that migration.

How do you apply for FSSAI on FoSCoS?

  1. Go to foscos.fssai.gov.in and sign up with the owner's mobile number and email.
  2. Choose your state and the kind of business (food service / restaurant). Enter expected turnover so the portal picks the right category.
  3. Fill in premises details, food categories and the name you will trade under.
  4. Upload photo ID, premises proof (rent agreement or ownership papers) and any extra documents your category asks for. A state licence usually asks for more, such as a kitchen layout and equipment list.
  5. Pay the fee online and note the application reference number.
  6. Answer any query or inspection notice quickly. Most delays we see come from address mismatches between the rent agreement, utility bill and application.

When does a cloud kitchen need GST registration?

Since 1 January 2022, Swiggy and Zomato pay GST on restaurant service supplied through their apps. CBIC's Circular 167/23/2021-GST says the platform pays even when the restaurant is unregistered. It also says those app sales count towards your aggregate turnover for the registration threshold.

For service businesses the threshold is ₹20 lakh of aggregate turnover in most states, lower in a few special category states. That is about ₹1.67 lakh of sales a month, which many kitchens cross within months. You also charge GST yourself on direct orders, such as takeaway or your own website. Shop and establishment, trade and health licences

The shop and establishment law is a state law, so the rules depend on where your kitchen is. In Maharashtra, an ordinance promulgated on 1 October 2025 raised the registration threshold from 10 to 20 workers, according to Khaitan & Co. Smaller establishments give an intimation instead. Other states have their own thresholds and portals.

The municipal trade or health licence is separate. It is issued by your city corporation, and many cities link it to your FSSAI registration, fire safety and property papers.

Fire NOC, pollution consent and signage: when do they apply?

Fire NOC. Requirements depend on state fire rules, the type of building, floor area, height and how much LPG you store. Ask your landlord for the building's fire NOC before you sign the lease, and check with the local fire office whether your kitchen needs its own.

Pollution consent. State Pollution Control Boards decide which food businesses need Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate. Size, a diesel generator, chimney exhaust and wastewater are what usually trigger it. Check your SPCB's category list rather than assuming you are exempt.

Signage. Some cities need permission for outdoor signs, and some states have language rules. In Karnataka, a 2024 law requires 60% of name boards to be in Kannada. Enforcement is under a High Court interim order: in March 2024 the court told the state not to seal shops for non-compliance, as Bar and Bench reported. Check the current position before you print your board.

Should you trade mark your brand name?

Yes, if you plan to grow the brand. Two kitchens in the same city with the same name on Swiggy is a common fight, and a registered trade mark makes it far easier to act. Restaurant services fall in Class 43. If you sell packaged sauces or snacks, you may need other classes too.

The official e-filing fee is ₹4,500 per class per mark for individuals, startups and small enterprises, and ₹9,000 for others, per the IP India fee schedule. Run a search on the IP India portal before you design your logo and packaging. Renaming later costs far more.

Cloud kitchen registration checklist

  1. Pick the legal structure (proprietorship, partnership, LLP or company) and get its PAN.
  2. Open a bank account in the business name. Platform payouts go here.
  3. Sign a rent agreement that allows commercial food use. Get the building fire NOC and occupancy papers from the landlord.
  4. Apply for FSSAI on FoSCoS in the right category for your expected turnover.
  5. Register or file an intimation under your state's shop and establishment law.
  6. Apply for the municipal trade or health licence your city requires.
  7. Register for GST if you will cross ₹20 lakh, or if you want a GSTIN from day one.
  8. Check fire, pollution and signage rules for your building and city.
  9. Search and file a Class 43 trade mark for your brand name.
  10. Keep every certificate as a clear PDF. You will upload them again and again.

Frequently asked questions

Is FSSAI registration enough to start a cloud kitchen?

No. FSSAI covers food safety, but you usually also need a shop and establishment registration or intimation under your state's law and a municipal trade or health licence in most cities. GST becomes compulsory once aggregate turnover crosses ₹20 lakh in most states. Fire and pollution approvals depend on your building and size. FSSAI is the minimum to list on Swiggy and Zomato, not the full set.

Which FSSAI category does a new cloud kitchen need in 2026?

From 1 April 2026, basic registration covers food businesses with annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore. A state licence applies above ₹1.5 crore up to ₹50 crore, and a central licence above ₹50 crore. Most new single-kitchen operations start with basic registration. If you expect to cross ₹1.5 crore in your first year, apply for the state licence directly to avoid a later upgrade.

Do I need GST if Swiggy and Zomato already pay GST on my orders?

Possibly. Since January 2022, the platforms pay GST on restaurant service sold through them, even for unregistered restaurants. But CBIC has clarified that those sales still count in your aggregate turnover. Once that crosses ₹20 lakh in most states, registration is required. Direct orders, such as takeaway or your own website, are taxed by you, so registering early is often simpler.

Can I run a cloud kitchen from home with these licences?

Yes, many home kitchens list on Swiggy and Zomato with FSSAI basic registration, since turnover is usually below ₹1.5 crore. You still need to check local rules on running a commercial activity from a residential address, and your housing society may have its own conditions. The platforms set their own onboarding checks. Our home kitchen guide covers the specific steps and limits.

How long does cloud kitchen registration take end to end?

FSSAI rules give 7 days to grant a registration or order an inspection, and 60 days for a licence after a complete application. Shop and establishment, trade licence and GST usually add a few weeks, run in parallel. Fire NOC is the wild card if your building needs one. Plan for several weeks, not days. Address mismatches cause most delays.
